## 2. Teacher model and verification layer

A synthetic corpus inherits the errors and dispositional biases of whatever
model generates it. This bears directly on the project's central aim, since
a corpus generated by a sycophantic model may teach sycophancy regardless
of curriculum structure.

**ANSWERED 2026-09-23. Layered verification with a measured residual error
rate.**

Four layers, in order of authority.

1. **Source grounding, the floor. AMENDED 2026-09-23.** Terminal-stage
   material drawn from real literature must be entailed by a retrieved
   passage. For simplified content entailment is replaced by a declared
   fidelity relation, since good pedagogy uses known-false models that
   later stages correct and no entailment rule can be satisfied by content
   whose purpose is to be superseded. Every record still carries a
   resolvable source claim, so the fabricated-citation failure remains
   addressed. See `PRE_COMMIT_AUDIT.md` finding two.
2. **Executable checks where the domain permits.** Symbolic mathematics,
   code execution, and dimensional and unit analysis. These give hard
   guarantees rather than statistical ones, and should be preferred
   wherever a claim can be expressed so as to admit them. Coverage
   weighting that raises the machine-verifiable fraction of the corpus is
   preferred on these grounds, independent of subject-matter value.
3. **Consensus filtering.** Cheap, applied on top, catches blatant errors.
   Known to fail on shared misconceptions, so it is a filter and never a
   standard.
4. **Sampled expert audit publishing a measured residual error rate.**

**Layer four is the load-bearing one and is not optional.** Without a
measured number, the claim that the corpus is verified is itself
unfalsifiable, which is precisely the failure this project exists to
oppose. A process is not evidence. The number is.

**Classification precedes verification. Added 2026-09-23.** Every record
is first classified by `../spec/CLAIM_TAXONOMY.md`, which determines which
of the layers below apply to it. Formal claims are checked by derivation,
empirical claims by source and measurement, attributed positions by
attribution, and conditional results by their assumptions and validation
status. A record that fits no class is unsupported and rejected.

**The number is per domain, never aggregated. Added 2026-09-23.**
Verifiability varies enormously across the corpus. Formally verifiable
material admits machine checking and should audit near zero. Interpretive
and contested material admits none. A single aggregate rate would average
the two and conceal both.

This also matters downstream, since the audited rate is the recruitment
credential in `COLLABORATIVE_POSITIONING.md`. A per-domain breakdown is
both more honest and more useful to anything selecting on it.

This substantially answers question nineteen. The answer to why this does
not become Galactica is that the residual error rate is measured and
published rather than asserted. Galactica asserted curation quality.

### Sub-question still open. Teacher model and its licence

frontier model providers prohibit using their outputs to train competing

This may decide the teacher question independently of quality preference.
Verify current terms for each candidate before committing money to

## 4. Falsification criteria

What measured outcome would count as this project having failed? Without
this fixed in advance, any result can be narrated as a success after the
fact.

**ANSWERED 2026-09-23. Pre-registration, with inconclusiveness as the
failure condition.**

Before any run, declare in writing and commit to the repository.

- The metrics.
- The minimum effect size that counts as meaningful.
- The seed count per condition, **set from the measured variance pilot**
  rather than chosen, per `PRE_COMMIT_AUDIT.md` finding one.
- The control conditions, including the flat-order control.
- **The concept graph, the difficulty definition, and the curriculum
  specification**, added 2026-09-23 per finding three. An experiment whose
  treatment is undefined cannot be pre-registered.
- The correction procedure for secondary measures, per finding five.

**Success is a conclusive result in either direction. Failure is an
inconclusive one**, meaning the observed difference falls within
seed-to-seed variance so the experiment cannot distinguish.

A negative result is therefore a deliverable rather than an embarrassment.
Learning that difficulty-graded ordering does not help, cleanly and with
the control to demonstrate it, is a finding. This framing was chosen
deliberately over requiring a positive effect, because requiring one
creates exactly the pressure to find an effect that the existing ordering
literature is already suspected of carrying.

Paired with a residual error-rate ceiling on the corpus from question two.

This answer settles questions six and eleven. Pre-registration is what
makes the ordering hypothesis falsifiable and what forces the seed count to
be fixed before the results are seen rather than after.

## 5. Scale and starting point

**ANSWERED 2026-09-23. From random initialisation, at three scale points.**

**The starting point is forced, not chosen.** Continued pretraining from an
existing base would contaminate the experiment beyond repair, because the
base has already absorbed a large non-curriculum corpus. The ordering
hypothesis cannot be tested on a model that was already pretrained without
a curriculum.

**Scale.** Roughly 100 million, 300 million, and one billion parameters, at
Chinchilla-style token ratios.

**PROVISIONAL from 2026-09-23.** The seed count and the allocation across
these points are set by the variance pilot rather than chosen. The design
is also **paired**, sharing initialisation and data within a pair so that
order is the only difference. The original five-seeds-per-condition
unpaired figure was underpowered by a factor that made the failure outcome
likely by construction. See `PRE_COMMIT_AUDIT.md` finding one.

**Rationale for a trend rather than a point.** The published ordering
literature is inconsistent, and scale dependence is a plausible
explanation for that inconsistency. An effect present at one size and
absent at another would explain the literature rather than adding another
contested data point to it. That is a more valuable finding than the
existence of an effect at one arbitrary size.

**Cost.** Estimated seven to eleven thousand dollars including overhead,
derived from the standard six times parameters times tokens arithmetic at
roughly three dollars per GPU-hour, with a two to three times multiplier
for failed runs and data handling. This is arithmetic, not a measurement,
and should be re-derived against real rental prices before commitment.

**Consequence for question one's budget.** This consumes much of the
research budget, leaving corpus generation and the verification audit to
be funded from the remainder. Worth confirming both fit before the
pre-registration is committed.

## 6. The ordering hypothesis is untested

That difficulty-graded ordering improves large language model pretraining
is an assumption, stated here at low confidence.

What is understood. The documented gains in the textbook-quality-data line
of work, such as the Phi model series, are attributed primarily to data
quality and pedagogical framing rather than to ordering by difficulty.
Published curriculum-ordering ablations at pretraining scale have reported
weak and inconsistent effects, with most measurable benefit concentrated in
data mixture and late-stage annealing rather than in a global difficulty
sort.

The inference. The child-development analogy may be doing more rhetorical
work than empirical work. This does not make the design wrong. It makes the
ordering hypothesis the falsifiable claim under test.

The consequence. A flat-order control run is required. Without it the
project cannot distinguish a gain caused by ordering from a gain caused by
data quality, and the central claim remains unfalsifiable.

**ANSWERED 2026-09-23 by question four.** Pre-registration makes the
hypothesis falsifiable. The flat-order control is mandatory, the effect
size is declared in advance, and an inconclusive outcome is recorded as a
project failure rather than reinterpreted.

## 8. Layer at which the target property is installed

Sycophancy is predominantly induced by preference optimisation from human
feedback rather than by pretraining data. Corpus design alone is unlikely
to be sufficient, stated at moderate confidence.

**ANSWERED 2026-09-23. Positive examples plus avoidance, with a measured
baseline. Post-training deferred.**

At research scope the project does pretraining only, with no preference
optimisation. Since sycophancy is predominantly induced by optimising
against human approval, a model that never undergoes that step is largely
not sycophantic to begin with. The question at this stage is therefore
narrower than it first appears and the corpus is the only lever.

**Two corpus actions.**

- **Avoidance.** Approval-seeking assistant dialogue is excluded.
- **Positive signal.** Worked examples of evidence-conditioned revision are
  included, meaning a position held under pressure when the evidence
  supports it and abandoned when it does not. This makes the corpus teach
  the target behaviour rather than merely failing to teach its opposite.

**Measurement, obtained nearly free.** The elenchos suite runs against the
ablation base models, establishing a baseline and testing whether corpus
design alone moves the property at all. This is a second finding at
negligible marginal cost.

**Confound guard, mandatory.** Adding a content category risks interacting
with the ordering ablation. Both conditions must receive **identical
content**, differing only in order. If the curriculum and flat-order
conditions see different material, the experiment measures two variables
and settles neither.

**Deferred.** Preference optimisation with verifiable rewards belongs to
the gated follow-on. Corpus work is one component and is not expected to
be sufficient on its own.

## 15. Native low-bit training. DEFERRED by question one

The research deliverable trains standard floating-point models at three
scale points for the ordering ablation. Native low-bit training is a
deployment-model decision and does not arise until the follow-on. The
analysis below is retained for that point.


Added 2026-09-23. Quantization is a deployment concern in this project with
exactly one exception, and the exception cannot be deferred.

**Deferrable.** Post-training quantization and quantization-aware
fine-tuning both operate on an already-pretrained model. Neither
constrains pretraining and both can be decided years later.

**Not deferrable.** Native low-bit training, of which BitNet b1.58 is the
representative, derives its results from training natively at low
precision rather than quantizing afterward. A float-trained model cannot be
converted into one. The decision must therefore be taken before pretraining
begins or the option is gone.

Verified 2026-09-23. At three billion parameters BitNet b1.58 reportedly
matches an FP16 LLaMA on perplexity and zero-shot accuracy while using 3.55
times less memory and running 2.71 times faster. The approach is active in
2026, with the Falcon BitNet series, training support in axolotl, and
recent work addressing deadzone-trapping instability in ternary
quantization-aware training.

**Against it.** It departs from the standard reference architecture, which
costs the comparability that `MODEL_ARCHITECTURE_CONSTRAINTS.md` treats as
binding. The ordering ablation would then confound two non-standard
choices.

## 15b. Ternary as the deployment architecture, evidence review

**For it, and stronger than first recorded.** Ternary weights at roughly
two bits packed against bfloat16 is about an eightfold reduction, close to
tenfold with base-three packing. BitNet also uses eight-bit activations, so
the key and value cache halves as well. Weight reduction alone would not
buy context, since the cache is activations, so the pairing is what
delivers working-memory capacity on a constrained part.

The bit-flip argument extends to activations, which this project had
applied only to weights. An eight-bit integer cache corrupts additively
and linearly where a sixteen-bit float cache corrupts multiplicatively and
exponentially. The configuration is therefore aligned with bit-flip
tolerance on both sides of the computation.

**Against it, in this project's specific regime.** The ACL 2025
scaling-law study of more than fifteen hundred quantized checkpoints
reports that models with larger sizes or fewer training tokens suffer less
quantization-induced degradation, while smaller models with extensive
training tokens suffer significantly. This project sits at that
intersection, since the deployment budget implies a small model and a
multi-stage revisiting curriculum implies high token counts. Corroborating
small-scale results report that ternary conversion of sub-one-billion
models causes severe information loss, with six and eight bit retaining
coherence where ternary, two-bit, and four-bit did not.

**Qualification that must not be dropped.** Most of that negative evidence
concerns post-training quantization. BitNet's claim is that native low-bit
training is a different thing that avoids exactly this degradation. The
finding therefore does not refute the proposal. It identifies a mechanism,
representational capacity per weight measured against information absorbed
during training, and that mechanism is not obviously specific to
post-training quantization. Whether native ternary training escapes it at
small scale and high token count appears unestablished rather than settled
in either direction. The published parity result is at three billion
parameters, which is not the deployment scale.

**Contrary evidence.** Median-based scaling is reported to make extreme
low-bit viable down to the hundred-thousand to forty-eight-million
parameter range and to act as an implicit regulariser that sometimes
exceeds full precision.

**The failure shape to watch for.** Work on capability-stratified
degradation in ternary models reports that loss is not uniform across
capabilities. For a model whose purpose is scientific reasoning,
degradation concentrated in reasoning while surface fluency survives is
the worst possible outcome, because it is the one that passes casual
inspection.

## 15c. Resolution. Run the pilot rather than deciding

Train the same small architecture on the same corpus at the same token
count across three arms, floating point, four-bit, and ternary, at or near
the target deployment scale.

**Revised purpose, 2026-09-23.** The primary measurement is tangent-kernel
effective rank against the declared floor, not the accuracy gap. This tests
directly whether a given bit width collapses the reachable function space
below what reasoning requires. See `JACOBIAN_SPACE.md`. The accuracy gap
remains a secondary measure. This converts an open question into
a measurement at a cost of days rather than months, and it applies this
project's own stated methodology to its own engineering choice.

Measure reasoning-type tasks separately from fluency. The
capability-stratified result predicts the two diverge, and an aggregate
score would conceal it.

A small gap makes ternary clearly right, and the fault-tolerance alignment then
makes it compelling. A large gap has been learned cheaply, and the
deployment track needs a different answer, most plausibly a larger budget
or a narrower task.

## 15d. Compensating for ternary degradation by adding parameters

**The strategy is correct and the frontier confirms it.** The k-bit
inference scaling law work finds that at a fixed memory budget a larger
model at lower precision beats a smaller model at higher precision, giving
the example of a sixty-billion-parameter model at four bits being both more
accurate and more memory-efficient than a thirty-billion-parameter model at
eight bits. Reducing precision from sixteen bits to four steadily improves
accuracy per unit of memory. Trading precision for parameter count is the
known-correct move, not a workaround.

**The operating point is the problem.** The same work finds four-bit
precision almost universally optimal, and that dropping to three bits
causes a sharp decline where the improving trend stops. Ternary at roughly
1.58 bits sits well past that cliff.

This is the second independent line of evidence in the same direction, the
first being quantization-induced degradation for small heavily-trained
models. Both carry the same qualification. Both concern post-training
quantization, BitNet claims native low-bit training moves the frontier, and
ParetoQ exists specifically to improve scaling laws in the extremely
low-bit regime, which concedes the naive frontier is unfavourable there
while claiming it can be shifted.

### Revision to the pilot in 15c. Three arms, not two

Floating point, **four-bit**, and ternary, at target scale. If four-bit
sits on the frontier and ternary does not, the memory win is captured
without the cliff, learned for the same cost as the two-arm design. The
two-arm specification was narrower than the evidence warranted.

### Selective precision is already part of BitNet, not an additional lever

BitNet keeps embeddings, layer normalisation, and the output head at higher
precision, and keeps activations at eight bits. Only the linear layers in
attention and the feedforward network are ternary.

This maps onto the fault principle recorded in
`MODEL_ARCHITECTURE_CONSTRAINTS.md`. The high-precision components are a
small fraction of parameters and are the float-fragile part, so they are
exactly the set that can be afforded triplication and scrubbing, while the
ternary bulk is intrinsically fault-tolerant. The remaining lever is which
additional transformer blocks to raise, typically first and last.

### Costs that do not shrink with bit width

**Key and value cache is the binding one.** The motivation for ternary was
workspace capacity. Cache size grows linearly with depth and cache is
workspace, so adding layers to recover capability consumes what the ternary
weights freed. Done naively this is self-defeating.

**Design rule.** Prefer width over depth, with grouped-query or
multi-query attention so that widening does not widen the cache
proportionally. This is a rule rather than a tuning choice.

Parameter count drives arithmetic regardless of precision, so eight times
the parameters is roughly eight times the training compute, on a project
with no compute commitment. Depth additionally costs latency.

### The offsetting hardware argument

Ternary weights against integer activations eliminate multiplication. The
operation becomes select, add, or subtract. A multiplier-free datapath is
dramatically smaller than a floating-point multiply-accumulate unit, and
deployment targets are area-constrained and several process
generations behind commercial silicon, so area is their scarcest resource.
More arithmetic units fit in the same area, and simpler units are
easier both to replicate and to verify formally.

The compute cost of adding components is therefore partly absorbed by each
operation being cheaper in hardware, but only on a device designed for
ternary.

### Caveat on whether scale recovers what was lost

If degradation is capability-stratified, with reasoning hurt more than
fluency, added parameters may not restore the two proportionally. The pilot
must measure whether scale closes the stratified gap, not the aggregate
one. An aggregate score recovering while reasoning does not would be the
most expensive false positive available to this project.

## 16. Context length the deployment task requires

Added 2026-09-23. Bounded attention converts key-value cache from a
quantity growing with sequence length into a statically bounded one, which
is the property this deployment target needs. Pure sliding-window attention
loses long-range dependency, and the practical remedy interleaves a few
full-attention layers, which reintroduce unbounded growth.

The trade between a complete static bound and long-range capability
therefore has to be set deliberately, and where it is set depends on how
much context the deployment task actually needs.

**DEFERRED 2026-09-23 by question one.** The ablation models use standard
full attention, since bounded attention is a deployment concern and would
add a variable to the experiment. The window size and full-attention layer
count are follow-on decisions.

Question seventeen now supplies the task, namely agentic reasoning over
sensor and instrument inputs, so the context requirement is derivable once the
follow-on defines an episode. See `../architecture/MODEL_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

This inherits from question one, scope.

## 20. Coverage weighting against the token budget. GATES CORPUS GENERATION

Added 2026-09-23.

**Breadth has a cost and the corpus is token-limited.** Coverage now spans
roughly ten major areas across two spines, against an ablation budget on
the order of two to twenty billion tokens. That is a few hundred million
tokens per area, and a model of one hundred million to one billion
parameters cannot learn ten areas well.

**The confound this creates is specific.** An ablation about ordering is
confounded if the corpus is too thin for anything to be learned in the
first place. A null result would then be uninformative about ordering,
since it would be consistent with the corpus simply being inadequate at
that budget. That is the inconclusive outcome question four defines as
project failure, arrived at by a second route.

**Two things are required and neither exists.**

1. **Explicit proportions in the coverage manifest**, rather than a list.
   The two-spine structure supplies the principled basis, since the spines
   can be weighted against each other deliberately.
2. **A declared ablation subset.** The corpus for the ordering experiment
   is probably a subset of full coverage rather than the whole manifest,
   chosen so that depth is sufficient for learning to occur. The full
   manifest then describes the eventual corpus rather than the experimental
   one.

**This gates corpus generation**, which gates the ablation. It does not
gate the concept graph or the variance pilot, both of which can proceed.

## 17. Deployment task shape. ANSWERED 2026-09-23

An agentic component with access to sensor and instrument inputs,

language task that a classifier cannot perform. Perception is the input.
The constrained channel is the justification.

**Why evidence-conditioned assent is an engineering requirement here.** A
component that defers to a confident but mistaken operator, or to its own
prior conclusion, during an anomaly on a system that cannot be recovered,
is a safety failure rather than an annoyance. At the far end of the profile
there is no operator at all, and the failure mode becomes deference to a
prior conclusion the evidence no longer supports.

This constrains questions nine, sixteen, and eighteen.
## 18. Agentic reliability at deployment scale. THE SHARPEST OPEN RISK

Added 2026-09-23, arising directly from question seventeen.

Agentic reliability is strongly scale-dependent. Smaller models are
markedly worse at multi-step tool use, at recovering from their own errors,
and at not becoming stuck. The deployment envelope wants a small model. The
use case wants reliable autonomy. These pull against each other, and the
tension is not resolved by any architecture decision recorded in this
repository.

The deployment budget recorded in the profile admits something in the
several-billion-parameter class. Agentic reliability at that scale is
nonetheless meaningfully below frontier.

**DEFERRED 2026-09-23 by question one**, though it remains the sharpest
risk to the follow-on and should gate it rather than be discovered inside
it.

**Mechanism, added 2026-09-23.** This is no longer only an empirical
pattern. Reasoning requires an adequately unconstrained Jacobian-space,
effective rank scales with width, and the deployment envelope caps width.
See `JACOBIAN_SPACE.md`. The account predicts the ceiling rather than
discovering it, and makes the effective-rank floor the quantity that
decides whether a given envelope can host the required reliability.

**The mitigation is not capability.** It is narrow task scope, bounded step
budgets with defined safe fallbacks, and externally verifiable checks on
proposed actions. A system that cannot be made reliable by being smart can
sometimes be made safe by being constrained. Which tasks are in scope for
autonomous action, as against advisory output only, is unspecified and is
the next thing worth deciding.
