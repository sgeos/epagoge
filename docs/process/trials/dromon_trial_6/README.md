# Sixth Dromon trial outputs

Recorded 2026-10-30. These files were written by the Dromon lines of the
sixth multi-agent trial under `tmp/dromon-trial-7/` and are copied here so
that a later commit can track them. Two edits were made for the gate's
reference check. In `review-part-a.md` two source file names were given
their directory, and in `harness-report-worker.md` the name of a scratch
script lost its code formatting. They are the lines' own
words and are evidence, not instructions. Their paths under `tmp/` refer
to ignored files that exist only on the operator's host. The reviewer's
files call this the seventh trial, after the scratch directory's number.
It is the sixth.

`readiness-terms.md` and `readiness_terms.py` are the luna line's readiness
report for the 26 level-two schedule units that have no book, counted from
the term entries of `curriculum/vocabulary.json` at levels one and two.
The reports of the third to fifth trials counted dictionary records in the
seed books and missed the level-one book `bk.dictionary.1.md`, so they
reported concepts as empty that have level-one words. Counted from the
term entries, no concept in those units is empty. The thinnest have one
word each. An inflection with its own term entry, such as holds beside
hold, counts as a separate word, and the reviewer found that folding such
entries changes no top-five position. A count measures vocabulary on file,
not whether a module can be written. The script passes the project's ruff
checks and is not type-checked, because the project's pyright
configuration does not include `docs/`.

`review-part-a.md` reviews the batch of suffix-stripped strings and the
operator-chosen tool change that lets a second sense of an admitted word
be filed. Its findings A, C and D were fixed afterwards. The verb sense of
flood was removed and flooding deferred. The provenance of color, cool and
well was restored and second senses were given their own map. The closure
check was made to check every dictionary record. The reviewer did not
review those fixes. The operator's session checked the provenance restore,
the flood removal, and that the closure check fails on an unlicensed word
planted in a second-sense record. `review.md` adds the review of luna's
report.

`harness-report-worker.md`, `harness-report-luna.md` and
`harness-report-reviewer.md` are each line's report on its experience of
the harness.
