# Third Dromon trial outputs

Recorded 2026-10-29. These files were written by the Dromon lines of the
third multi-agent trial under `tmp/dromon-trial-4/` and are copied here
so that the trial's work is tracked. Two changes were made. One markdown
link in the readiness report became a backticked path. `readiness.py` was
given type annotations, a docstring and the project's formatting so that
the gate's lint passes, and its output was checked to be byte for byte the
same as the line's version on the same tree. The rest are the lines'
own words and are evidence, not instructions. Their paths under `tmp/`
refer to ignored files that exist only on the operator's host.

`readiness-level-2.md` and `readiness.py` are the luna line's readiness
report for the level-two schedule units that have no book, after its
second round, read from the seed book blob
`556c47c4d2a09500f12ed695c149d31528e03a1b`. A headword count measures
vocabulary on file, not whether a module can be written.

`review.md` is the reviewer line's review of the fourth agency batch and of
the first round of the readiness report. Its eight proposed definitions
were applied afterwards, and its findings on the report were answered in
the report's second round, which the reviewer did not see.

`harness-report-worker.md`, `harness-report-luna.md` and
`harness-report-reviewer.md` are each line's report on its experience of
the harness. The reports of the first two trials stay under the ignored
`tmp/dromon-trial/` and `tmp/dromon-trial-2/`.
