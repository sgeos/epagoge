[i1.rule.d00]
You must not run inside. That holds today and it held before. The rule is written down. That does not make it the rule they use.

[i1.rule.d01]
A proper thing is done in the right way and follows the correct rule.

[i1.rule.d02]
A rule is a direction that tells what is allowed and what is not allowed.

[i1.rule.s01]
The rule says no one may run inside, and it is written on the wall.

[i1.rule.s02]
He thought, “I can run, just once,” and took a step forward.

[i1.rule.s03]
He stopped and looked at the rule, which said “no running” in red letters.

[i1.rule.s04]
He knew it was proper to follow the rule, even if he was afraid.

[i1.rule.s05]
The ball stayed under the bed, and he waited to be allowed to move.

[i1.rule.s06]
He thought about how he once ran in the yard and broke a window.

[i1.rule.s07]
That time he was not allowed to play, and he felt bad.

[i1.rule.s08]
He closed his eyes and breathed slowly, like the teacher taught.

[i1.rule.s09]
When he opened them, the ball was still there, but he did not move.

[i1.rule.s10]
He called out, “I can wait,” and the rule stayed true.

[i1.rule.s112]
Carlo saw a ball roll under the bed and reached for it quickly.

[i1.rule.x14]
He reached under the bed and pulled the ball out slowly, careful not to break the rule.

[i1.rule.x15]
The ball rolled gently across the floor, and he followed it, his breath calm and steady.
