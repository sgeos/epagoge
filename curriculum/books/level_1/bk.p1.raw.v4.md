[p1.raw.v4.s01]
A tree grew in the forest, natural and alive. It stood tall above the others, reaching for the bright sky. The wind blew through its leaves, making a gentle sound. No one had built it, no one had changed it. It lived by itself, yet belonged to the forest. Its life was real, not made or copied.

[p1.raw.v4.x08]
The chair sat in the sun, warm and still. It had been built by hands, not grown from the earth. Its legs were made of wood, same as the tree that once stood. It had stood above the others, reaching for the sky. The sun warmed the wood, making it soft to touch.

[p1.raw.v4.x09]
A child sat on the chair, swinging her feet. The wood felt warm from the sun above. She reached down and picked up a stick. The stick was smooth, like a branch broken from a tree. She drew a circle in the dirt, then a line crossing it. She looked up, eyes bright, face full of thought.

[p1.raw.v4.x10]
She picked up a stick and drew shapes in the dirt. She held the stick and drew a line across the dirt. The stick felt alive in her hand, like a branch from a tree. The air above was warm and still, full of light. She looked at the shapes and wondered if they meant something.

[p1.raw.v4.x11]
A butterfly landed on her hand, its wings bright blue. She did not move, afraid to break the moment. The blue was deeper than sky, brighter than glass. It was a colour that meant something real. She thought of the tree that once stood in the clearing. The wood was a tree, and now it was a stick.

[p1.raw.v4.x12]
She whispered, “Stay,” and the butterfly flew away. She looked at the stick, still warm from the sun. It was a piece of the tree that once stood above the clearing. The wood had been alive, tall and strong, with branches that reached the sky. Now it was broken, lying on the ground like a secret.

[p1.raw.v4.x13]
A child sat on the chair, reading a book about trees. The pages showed a tree that once stood above the clearing. The wood had been alive, strong, and tall. Now it was broken, lying on the ground. She thought about the life it had lived. The tree had felt the wind, the rain, and the sun.

[p1.raw.v4.x14]
The book slipped from her hands, landing open on the dirt. She felt the weight of the book, warm in her hands. The pages showed a life once lived, now gone. Each mark on the paper told a story of being alive. The sun shone bright, but the air felt cold.

[p1.raw.v4.x15]
She pulled it close, opening it again to the first page. Each mark on the paper told a story of being alive. The sun shone bright, but the air felt cold. She felt the weight of the book, warm in her hands. The pages showed a life once lived, now gone.
