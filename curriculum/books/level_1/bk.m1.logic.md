If it rains then the ground is wet. All, some, none. It cannot be both so and not so.

[m1.logic.d01]
No person, not anyone, no one at all.

[m1.logic.d112]
To follow, to come after, to happen next.

The ground stays wet after the rain stops. Each step leaves a mark, and the dirt holds the shape of feet. The ground is not dry, not yet. It stays wet, soaked through and deep. Even the grass stays bent, heavy with water. No sun breaks through to warm the surface.

[m1.logic.x09]
She catches it with both hands. The balloon feels soft and warm from the sun. Rain had fallen earlier, so the ground is wet. She stands still, breathing slowly, not afraid. The sky above is bright blue, no clouds now. The wind has stopped, so the balloon floats gently. The branch is high, but she can reach it now.

She laughs and runs to her mother. The ground is wet from rain that fell earlier. She feels the cool mud under her shoes. Her mother bends down, arms open, and pulls her close. The sky above is bright blue, no clouds now. The balloon floats high, still drifting above the tree.

[m1.logic.x11]
They go home, dry off, and share a warm drink. The steam rises from the cup, warm and bright. The sky above is clear, no clouds to block the light. The ground stays wet from rain that fell earlier. She cannot play outside, not with mud on her shoes. The sun comes out, but the ground remains wet.

[m1.logic.x12]
The ground stays wet for a long time, even when the sun comes out. The sun shines, but it cannot dry everything at once. The ground waits, still holding the rain. It does not forget what fell. The earth is alive with quiet change. No one can rush it. The ground must dry in its own time.
