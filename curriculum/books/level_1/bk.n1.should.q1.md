[n1.should.q1.d00]
You should not run into the street. You should hold the handle.

[n1.should.q1.d01]
You must do something because it is the right thing to do.

[n1.should.q1.a01]
Should you run into the street? No, you should not run into the street.

[n1.should.q1.a02]
What should you do instead? You should wait for a safe time to cross.

[n1.should.q1.a03]
What should you do before crossing? You should look both ways and check for cars.

[n1.should.q1.a04]
What should you do when you see a car coming? You should wait and not cross.

[n1.should.q1.a05]
What should you do when it is safe to cross? You should walk across the street slowly.

[n1.should.q1.a06]
Why should you hold the handle? You should hold the handle so you do not fall or get lost.

[n1.should.q1.a07]
What should you do if you drop the handle? You should stop and pick it up right away.

[n1.should.q1.a08]
What should you do if someone tells you to run into the street? You should not run; you should say no and stay safe.

[n1.should.q1.a09]
What should you remember every time you cross the street? You should remember: stop, look, and cross only when safe.

[n1.should.q1.a10]
What should you do if you need to cross the street? You should wait for the light to turn green.

[n1.should.q1.a11]
What should you do when the light is green? You should look both ways before you cross.

[n1.should.q1.a12]
What should you do if a car is coming? You should stop and wait until the car passes.

[n1.should.q1.a13]
What should you do if you see a balloon floating into the street? You should not run after it.

[n1.should.q1.a14]
What should you do with a toy that rolls into the street? You should not run after it.
