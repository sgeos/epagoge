[p1.touch.v2.d00]
Your hand is on the cup. Nothing moves until something touches it.

[p1.touch.v2.d01]
Contact means when one thing meets another without breaking apart.

[p1.touch.v2.d02]
Patted means to touch something gently with the hand, often to show care.

[p1.touch.v2.d03]
Pressed means to push something with force, often to make it fit or stay in place.

[p1.touch.v2.d04]
Touch means to come into contact with something, using a finger, hand, or body part.
