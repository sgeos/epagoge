If it rains then the ground is wet. All, some, none. It cannot be both so and not so.

[m1.logic.x_goal.s01]
A goal is to aim, want, and try, but nobody can do it all, none can do nothing, and nothing is not real. Each person has a dream, a goal, a reason to keep going. Even when afraid, one must go ahead, not back or away. It is real, like air, like light, like love.

A boy wanted to play, but he knew he must go inside, and no one else wanted to go either. A boy sat alone, feeling sad, because no one would play. He thought about the goal, but it felt far, like a dream. He looked around, seeing nothing but walls and light, no path, no way.

[m1.logic.x_goal.s04]
He tried to find a way, but there was no path, no door, no place to go, and nothing to do. He saw a small light above, bright and warm, like a star. He wanted to go ahead, but was afraid of the dark path. He thought about the goal, now clear, not a dream.
