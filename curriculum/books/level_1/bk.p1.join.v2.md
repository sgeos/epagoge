This book is about putting the lid on the pot and taking the lid off the pot.

[p1.join.v2.d01]
Apart means separated, not joined together.

[p1.join.v2.d02]
Glue joins things together.

[p1.join.v2.d03]
Separate means to pull things apart.

[p1.join.v2.s01]
The pot was empty and the lid was apart from it. The lid was above the pot and looked ready to fit. I reached out and pulled it down. The edge of the lid matched the rim of the pot. I pressed it gently to close. The lid stayed in place, not moving or falling.

[p1.join.v2.s02]
I used glue to join the lid to the pot. The glue stayed strong and held the lid in place. No air could get in or out. The lid would not move, even when I shook the pot. I placed it on the table, safe and still. No one could take it off.

The lid stayed joined because the glue was strong. No force could break it, not even a strong pull. I tried to lift it, but it would not move. The glue held it fast, like a secret locked in place. The lid stayed put, safe above the pot. No air could get in or out.
