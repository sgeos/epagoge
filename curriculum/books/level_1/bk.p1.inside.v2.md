[p1.inside.v2.d00]
The water is in the cup. Now it is out of the cup.

[p1.inside.v2.d01]
A thing that holds something inside, like a cup or a box.

[p1.inside.v2.d02]
To go inside something, like a door or a room.

[p1.inside.v2.d03]
To be the right size, not too big or too small, for something.

[p1.inside.v2.d04]
The part of something that is not on the outside, like water inside a cup.

[p1.inside.v2.d05]
The part of something that is not inside, like the side of a cup you can touch.

[p1.inside.v2.s01]
The cup is a container that fits water inside. The water is in the cup. The cup sits on the table. The table is flat and made of wood. The water feels cool to the touch. The cup has a handle. The handle is smooth and round. The water is clear and liquid.

[p1.inside.v2.x07]
The water spills onto the table. The table is wet and dark where the water spread. The handle feels cold and smooth. The water did not stay inside the cup. The spill moved across the flat wood. The liquid flowed to the edge and dropped down. The floor is below and ready to take the drop.

[p1.inside.v2.x08]
The cup is empty and sits on the floor. No water remains inside the cup. The floor is wet and dark where it fell. The handle is cold and smooth. The cup does not move. It stays still on the wood. The air is quiet above the spill. No one comes to clean it.

[p1.inside.v2.x09]
The water spilled on the floor. The water is in the cup. It sits on the table, still and full. The cup is warm from the sun above. No one moves near it. The air is calm, no breeze. The cup holds the water, safe and whole. It does not fall, does not spill.

[p1.inside.v2.x10]
It made a small puddle near the door. The puddle spread a little, dark and still. No one came to clean it, not yet. The dog sniffed the wet spot. The air was quiet, full of dust and time. The cup stayed full, safe on the table. The sun moved slowly, high and bright.

[p1.inside.v2.x11]
The dog sniffed the wet spot. The spot smelled like water and earth. The air above the floor was still. The cup stayed full, bright and clean. The water inside did not move. The dog lifted his head. He looked at the cup. He wanted to drink but could not reach.

[p1.inside.v2.x12]
The puddle grew bigger where he walked. The air above stayed still. The cup stayed full, bright and clean. The water inside did not move. The dog lifted his head. He looked at the cup. He wanted to drink but could not reach. The puddle spread across the floor. It touched the corner of the table.

[p1.inside.v2.x13]
She sat on the table and watched. The water inside the cup stayed bright and clear. The air above was still and warm. She felt a soft breeze from the window. The sun shone through the glass, making the light sharp. The cup held the water, no more, no less.

[p1.inside.v2.x14]
The water kept flowing from the cup. It slipped down the side and touched the table. The surface below was dry, but the flow made a small wet mark. The cup was not empty, but it was losing. The table felt warm where the water landed. The cup now held less, but still had some.

[p1.inside.v2.x15]
It dripped down the edge and fell. The drop landed on the floor with a small splash. The floor was dry, but now it held a wet spot. The cup was still half full, but less than before. The water kept flowing, slow and quiet. It moved across the wood, a thin line of liquid.
