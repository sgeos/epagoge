[c1.what_you_saw.d00]
You saw him take it. You did not see why. Say what you saw.

[c1.what_you_saw.d01]
A report is a story or list of what happened, told to someone in charge.

[c1.what_you_saw.s01]
A boy saw a man take a red ball from a basket.

[c1.what_you_saw.s02]
The boy knew the ball was not his, but he did not know why the man took it.

[c1.what_you_saw.s03]
The boy felt afraid to speak, but he knew he must say what he saw.

[c1.what_you_saw.s04]
He used his voice to report what he saw.

[c1.what_you_saw.s05]
He wanted to be honest, not afraid of what might happen.

[c1.what_you_saw.s06]
His voice was calm, not loud or angry.

[c1.what_you_saw.s07]

[c1.what_you_saw.s08]
He did not blame anyone else, only told what he saw.

[c1.what_you_saw.s09]
He stood still, his hands at his sides, not moving.

[c1.what_you_saw.s10]

[c1.what_you_saw.s11]
