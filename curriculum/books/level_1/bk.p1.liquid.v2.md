[p1.liquid.v2.d00]
Water takes the shape of the cup. The empty cup is full of air.

[p1.liquid.v2.d01]
Blow means to push air out with your mouth.

[p1.liquid.v2.d02]
Breath is the air you push out when you blow or pull in when you breathe.

[p1.liquid.v2.d03]
Bubble is a round ball of air inside liquid.

[p1.liquid.v2.d04]
Drank means to drink something, like water or juice.

[p1.liquid.v2.d05]
Drip is a small drop of liquid falling slowly.

[p1.liquid.v2.s01]
The cup is empty and full of air. Air fills the space where water could be. No water is inside, only air. It flows out in a quiet stream. The cup remains empty and bright. Nothing changes the shape of the cup. The cup holds air, not liquid. It takes the shape of the cup.

[p1.liquid.v2.x07]
The cup sits on the table, full and bright. Water fills the space where air once stayed. The water takes the shape of the cup. It flows slowly, quiet and clear. The cup remains full, not empty. The surface is smooth, like glass. The light shines through, bright and blue.

[p1.liquid.v2.x08]
The child blows again, making another bubble. The bubble floats above the cup, round and bright. The child watches, eyes wide, breath held. The bubble grows, smooth and clear, like a tiny world. The child reaches, fingers just above, afraid to touch. It passes the window, where the sun is high.

[p1.liquid.v2.x09]
Water flows from the cup into the bowl. It fills the bottom, rising higher with each drop. The child watches, eyes wide, breath held. The water does not stay the same, it changes shape. The cup is empty, the bowl full, and the water is alive. It flows, it moves, it shapes itself to the space.

[p1.liquid.v2.x10]
The boy blows again, making more bubbles that float away. The bubbles float above the bowl, bright and round. Each one is alive, moving with the breeze. The boy watches, his eyes wide, breath held. He cannot catch them, though he tries to reach. The water in the bowl stays still, calm and clear.

[p1.liquid.v2.x11]
The cup is now empty, and the air inside feels cool. Water cannot stay in the cup if it is taken away. The cup holds nothing, but the air inside is alive. The air moves, though no one sees it. It flows above the bowl, through the room, and past the window.

[p1.liquid.v2.x12]
The sun shines through the window, warming the empty space. The air moves above the floor, soft and alive. A breeze drifts across the room, lifting the curtain. The light flows in, bright and clear. It touches the wall, then the table, then the floor. The cup lies still, empty and cool.

[p1.liquid.v2.x13]
The cup tips over and water spills onto the floor. The water spreads across the floor, flat and still. The light from the window bends above the spill. The floor feels cool where the water touches. The water remembers the shape of the cup. It fills every gap, every crack, every space.

[p1.liquid.v2.x14]
The spilled water flows across the wooden floor toward the door. It moves like a slow breath, soft and sure. The water finds every crack, each small gap, and fills them. The water does not stop, it only flows ahead. It climbs a small rise, then slides down again. The shape changes, but the water stays.

[p1.liquid.v2.x15]
The boy stands up, picks up the cup, and carries it to the sink. It fills the bowl, then spills over the edge, dripping down the side. The boy watches, his face calm, his hands still. The cup is empty, clean, and ready to be filled again. He turns the tap, and water rises, clear and bright.
