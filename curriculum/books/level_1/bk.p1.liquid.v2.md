[p1.liquid.v2.d00]
Water takes the shape of the cup. The empty cup is full of air.

[p1.liquid.v2.d01]
Blow means to push air out with your mouth.

[p1.liquid.v2.d02]
Breath is the air you push out when you blow or pull in when you breathe.

[p1.liquid.v2.d03]
Bubble is a round ball of air inside liquid.

[p1.liquid.v2.d04]
Drank means to drink something, like water or juice.

[p1.liquid.v2.d05]
Drip is a small drop of liquid falling slowly.

[p1.liquid.v2.s01]
The cup is empty and full of air.

[p1.liquid.v2.x07]
The cup sits on the table, full and bright.

[p1.liquid.v2.x08]
The child blows again, making another bubble.

[p1.liquid.v2.x09]

[p1.liquid.v2.x10]
The boy blows again, making more bubbles that float away.

[p1.liquid.v2.x11]
The cup is now empty, and the air inside feels cool.

[p1.liquid.v2.x12]
The sun shines through the window, warming the empty space.

[p1.liquid.v2.x13]

[p1.liquid.v2.x14]

[p1.liquid.v2.x15]
