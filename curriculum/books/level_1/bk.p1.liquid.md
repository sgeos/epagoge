[p1.liquid.d00]
Water takes the shape of the cup. The empty cup is full of air.

[p1.liquid.d01]
Blow means to push air out of the mouth with force.

[p1.liquid.d02]
Breath is the air that comes out when you blow.

[p1.liquid.d03]
A bubble is a round ball of air inside liquid.

[p1.liquid.d04]
Drank means to take liquid into the mouth and swallow it.

[p1.liquid.d05]
Drip is a small drop of liquid falling slowly.

[p1.liquid.s01]
The cup was empty, full of air.

[p1.liquid.x07]
The bubble floated above the cup.

[p1.liquid.x08]
The cup sat on the table, still empty.

[p1.liquid.x09]
A boy picked up the cup and held it close.

[p1.liquid.x10]
He blew into the cup, and air rushed out again.

[p1.liquid.x11]
The cup felt warm in his hands.

[p1.liquid.x12]
The bubble floated above the cup, bright and round.

[p1.liquid.x13]
The cup tipped, and the water dripped onto the floor.

[p1.liquid.x14]
The boy picked up the cup and carried it to the sink.

[p1.liquid.x15]
The cup still held air, waiting to be filled again.
