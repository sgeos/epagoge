[p1.touch.v4.d00]
Your hand is on the cup. Nothing moves until something touches it.

[p1.touch.v4.d01]
Contact means when one thing meets another, like a hand touching a cup.

[p1.touch.v4.d02]
Patted means to gently tap something with a hand, like a soft touch on a dog.

[p1.touch.v4.d03]
Pressed means to push down with force, making something move or change.

[p1.touch.v4.d04]
Rub means to move a hand over a surface, like rubbing a blanket.

[p1.touch.v4.d05]
Stroke means to move a hand gently over a surface or body part.

[p1.touch.v4.d06]
Touch means to have your hand meet something, like touching a warm cup.
