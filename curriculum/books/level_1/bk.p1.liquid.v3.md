[p1.liquid.v3.d00]
Water takes the shape of the cup. The empty cup is full of air.

[p1.liquid.v3.d01]
Blow means to push air out with your mouth or with wind.

[p1.liquid.v3.d02]
Breath is the air you take in and push out when you breathe.

[p1.liquid.v3.d03]
Bubble is a round shape made of liquid or air that floats on water.

[p1.liquid.v3.d04]
Drank means to take liquid into your mouth and swallow it.

[p1.liquid.v3.d05]
Drip is a small drop of liquid falling slowly.

[p1.liquid.v3.s01]
The cup is empty and full of air.

[p1.liquid.v3.x07]
The cup sits on the table, waiting to be filled.

[p1.liquid.v3.x08]
The cup is now full, and the water is still.

[p1.liquid.v3.x09]
The empty cup is placed on the shelf, near the window.

[p1.liquid.v3.x10]
The bubble grows, round and bright, floating above the cup.

[p1.liquid.v3.x11]
The cup tips, and the water spills, dripping down the side and onto the floor.

[p1.liquid.v3.x12]
The water fills the cup and spills over the edge, making a small puddle on the wood.

[p1.liquid.v3.x13]
The water spreads across the floor, soaking the wood.

[p1.liquid.v3.x14]
The sun shines through the window, warming the wet floor and drying the edge.

[p1.liquid.v3.x15]
The puddle spreads, soaking into the wood, dark and wide.
