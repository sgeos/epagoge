[p1.liquid.v3.d00]
Water takes the shape of the cup. The empty cup is full of air.

[p1.liquid.v3.d01]
Blow means to push air out with your mouth or with wind.

[p1.liquid.v3.d02]
Breath is the air you take in and push out when you breathe.

[p1.liquid.v3.d03]
Bubble is a round shape made of liquid or air that floats on water.

[p1.liquid.v3.d04]
Drank means to take liquid into your mouth and swallow it.

[p1.liquid.v3.d05]
Drip is a small drop of liquid falling slowly.

[p1.liquid.v3.s01]
The cup is empty and full of air. Water cannot live without a shape. It flows like a breeze above the table. No one can see it, but it is there. The cup holds the air, and the air fills the cup. The cup is not full, but it is ready.

[p1.liquid.v3.x07]
The cup sits on the table, waiting to be filled. Water cannot stay in one place without a shape. It flows above the table, smooth and bright. The air inside the cup is alive and ready. No one can see it, but it is there. The cup holds the air, and the air fills the cup.

[p1.liquid.v3.x08]
The cup is now full, and the water is still. It holds the same shape as the cup, no more, no less. The cup is full, and the water does not move. It stays, like a secret held in place. The air above the water is still too, alive but quiet.

[p1.liquid.v3.x09]
The empty cup is placed on the shelf, near the window. The light from outside shines through, bright and clear. The cup sits still, no water inside, no shape to hold. It is empty, and the space above it feels wide. The shelf is flat, the wood smooth and old.

[p1.liquid.v3.x10]
The bubble grows, round and bright, floating above the cup. The air around it feels still, as if holding its breath. It does not fall, though it is not held. The cup below remains still, empty and waiting. The bubble is not afraid, though it is alone. It does not know what comes next, but it keeps going.

[p1.liquid.v3.x11]
The cup tips, and the water spills, dripping down the side and onto the floor. It spreads slowly, like a secret told in whispers. Each drop leaves a mark, a tiny story in liquid. The air above grows thick with the smell of wet earth. The bubble above the cup is gone, forgotten.

[p1.liquid.v3.x12]
The water fills the cup and spills over the edge, making a small puddle on the wood. It spreads slowly, like a secret told in whispers. Each drop leaves a mark, a tiny story in liquid. The air above grows thick with the smell of wet earth. The bubble above the cup is gone, forgotten.

[p1.liquid.v3.x13]
The water spreads across the floor, soaking the wood. It flows like a secret told in whispers. Each drop leaves a mark, a tiny story in liquid. The air above grows thick with the smell of wet earth. The bubble above the cup is gone, forgotten. The floor feels cool, alive with hidden paths.

[p1.liquid.v3.x14]
The sun shines through the window, warming the wet floor and drying the edge. The floor feels cool, not dead, but alive with hidden stories. No cup is needed, no container, no rule. No one sees it, but it is there, moving, flowing, changing. The shape is not fixed, not true, not wrong.

[p1.liquid.v3.x15]
The puddle spreads, soaking into the wood, dark and wide. No shape is fixed, not even the one it wears now. The air above it is still, but the water breathes. It remembers the cup, though it never was held. It can be small or large, shallow or deep. It hides under the table, then rises again.
