[f1.parts.v3.d00]
A thing is made of parts. One bad part can stop the whole thing.

[f1.parts.v3.d01]
A part is a piece of something bigger that helps it work.

[f1.parts.v3.d02]
A whole is all of something, not broken into pieces.

[f1.parts.v3.s01]
A boy built a boat from wood and rope. A boat is made of parts that fit together. Each part helps the whole move across the water. The wood holds the shape and keeps it strong. The rope ties the parts so they do not come apart. The boy knew each part had a job to do.

[f1.parts.v3.s02]
The part was made of strong wood and tied tight. The boy knew it would not break. Each piece fit just right. No space was left between. The wood stayed straight and true. No crack or bend showed. The shape stayed strong. It could carry the weight of the boy.

[f1.parts.v3.s03]
The boy tested the part by pulling it hard. No crack appeared above the join. The wood stayed straight and true. The part was built to last. It could carry more than just his weight. Each piece fit as one. No space was left between. The shape stayed whole, no break or bend.

[f1.parts.v3.s04]
The part stayed in place and did not break. No space was left above or below the join. The wood stayed alive and true through every test. It could carry more than one weight at once. Each part worked as one, never apart. The shape stayed whole, no crack or bend.

[f1.parts.v3.s05]
The boat was a whole thing, built from many parts. The wood held strong, shaped by hands and time. No piece was left behind or thrown away. Its shape stayed true, not bent or broken. All parts worked as one, never apart. The boat stayed alive, moving ahead. The boat moved ahead through the water, shaped by parts that fit just right.
