[f1.parts.v3.d00]
A thing is made of parts. One bad part can stop the whole thing.

[f1.parts.v3.d01]
A part is a piece of something bigger that helps it work.

[f1.parts.v3.d02]
A whole is all of something, not broken into pieces.

[f1.parts.v3.s01]
A boy built a boat from wood and rope.

[f1.parts.v3.s02]
The part was made of strong wood and tied tight.

[f1.parts.v3.s03]
The boy tested the part by pulling it hard.

[f1.parts.v3.s04]

[f1.parts.v3.s05]

[f1.parts.v3.s06]

[f1.parts.v3.s07]

[f1.parts.v3.s08]
