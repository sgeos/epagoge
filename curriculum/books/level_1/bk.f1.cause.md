[f1.cause.d00]
You pushed it and it moved. The pushing came first.

[f1.cause.d01]
It shows the reason something happens, like when a ball rolls because you pushed it.

[f1.cause.d02]
It is what makes something happen, like the push that causes the ball to move.

[f1.cause.s01]
I pushed the ball because it was on the path. It rolled across the grass. The push caused the ball to move. I watched it go ahead. The ball rolled toward the tree. I followed it along the path. I reached down to pick it up. My hand touched the warm surface.

The push caused the ball to roll across the grass. It rolled fast, past the tree and into the dirt. I reached down to touch it, my fingers brushing the soft grass. The dirt felt rough, like tiny stones under my hand. I pulled it back, the ball rolling again, this time along the path.

It stopped near the house, and I picked it up, glad it was safe. It moved again, just a little, as if it knew I was watching. I held it close, not wanting to lose it. The sun shone bright above, warming my hand. I felt it breathe, soft and alive, like a secret.
