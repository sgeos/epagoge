[p1.raw.v3.d00]
A tree grew in the forest, and its wood was used to build a chair.

[p1.raw.v3.d01]
A thing that is baked has been cooked in an oven.

[p1.raw.v3.d02]
A thing that builds makes something strong and lasting.

[p1.raw.v3.d03]
A thing that was built was made by hands or tools.

[p1.raw.v3.d04]
To carve means to cut shape from wood or stone.

[p1.raw.v3.d05]
A thing that has grown has become bigger over time.

[p1.raw.v3.d06]
A natural thing comes from the earth, not made by people.

[p1.raw.v3.d07]
A raw thing has not been changed by fire or tool.
