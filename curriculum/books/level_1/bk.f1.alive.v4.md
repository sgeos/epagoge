[f1.alive.v4.d00]
Living things die. Dying does not go backwards.

[f1.alive.v4.d01]
Alive means able to live, breathe, and feel, not dead.

[f1.alive.v4.d02]
Asleep means resting with eyes closed, not awake.

[f1.alive.v4.d03]
A dead thing cannot eat, drink, move, or feel anything.

[f1.alive.v4.d04]
Death means the end of life, when a living thing stops living.

[f1.alive.v4.d05]
Die means to stop living, to become dead.

[f1.alive.v4.d06]
Drink means to take liquid into the mouth and swallow it.
