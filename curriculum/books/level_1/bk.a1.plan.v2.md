[a1.plan.v2.d00]
Before you do it, you think how. Then you do it that way.

[a1.plan.v2.d01]
A list is a group of things written down in order.

[a1.plan.v2.d02]
A method is a way of doing something.

[a1.plan.v2.d03]
A plan is a way of thinking ahead about what to do.

[a1.plan.v2.s01]
I saw a big apple on the ground and thought how to pick it up.

[a1.plan.v2.s02]
I decided to use a stick to reach it, because I was afraid to go near the tree.

[a1.plan.v2.s03]
I made a plan to use the stick and tested it on a small stone.

[a1.plan.v2.s04]
I found a long stick and used it to push the apple closer.

[a1.plan.v2.s05]
The stick slipped and the apple rolled across the grass.

[a1.plan.v2.s06]
I followed it and caught it with my hand, feeling happy.

[a1.plan.v2.s07]
I looked at the apple and thought about how to carry it home.

[a1.plan.v2.s08]
I used a basket to carry it, because it was heavy and I did not want to drop it.

[a1.plan.v2.s09]
I walked slowly, step by step, with the basket in my hand.

[a1.plan.v2.s10]
I thought about how to cross the bridge without falling.

[a1.plan.v2.s11]
I reached the other side and sat on a stone to rest.

[a1.plan.v2.s12]
I opened the basket and saw the apple was still whole and bright.
