[r1.where.v4.d01]
Above means higher than something else in space.

[r1.where.v4.d02]
Ahead means in the direction you are going.

[r1.where.v4.d03]
Backward means in the opposite direction of forward.

[r1.where.v4.d04]
Behind means at the back of something.

[r1.where.v4.d05]
Below means lower than something else in space.

[r1.where.v4.d06]
Beside means next to something.

[r1.where.v4.d07]
Direction means the way something is going.

[r1.where.v4.d08]
Following means coming after something.
