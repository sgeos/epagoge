[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.d00]
How do you know? Let us go and find out. Now we have settled it.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s01]
I want to discover how to find the truth, so I go with others to look for it. I am alive and able to see, hear, and feel. I walk with others, not alone, because I need their help. We look for truth, not just answers, and we share what we find.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s02]
I look alone, not with others, because I must find my own way. I am alone, not with others, because I must find my own truth. I feel the air, sharp and cold, above the ground. I walk slowly, not fast, along the path I made. I think about what I saw, what I heard, what I felt.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s03]
I look up, down, around, and even inside myself, to find the truth. I see my breath, a small cloud in the cold air above. I think about what I am, and what I could become. I wonder if others feel the same, or if I am alone in this.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s04]
I find a book that tells a story, and I read it to discover what is true. The story shows a boy who walks alone, yet feels close to others. He wonders if his thoughts are alike, or if each mind is different. He sees a bird fly across the sky and feels a pull inside.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s05]
I hear a voice that says, “You must trust yourself, not just others.” I see my breath in the cold air, like a small cloud of truth. I am able to choose my own path, not just follow. I can speak my mind, even if others disagree. I can be gentle even when I am angry.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s06]
I stand up, and I say, “I will know for myself, not just because others say so.” I know my own mind, not just what others say. I can feel my body alive, not just asleep. I stand tall, not bent by fear. I choose to speak, not stay quiet.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s07]
I am alone, but I look for myself, and I discover I am not alone. I see my hands, and I know they are mine. I feel my breath, and I know I am alive. I stand, and I feel my body, not just my mind. I see the sky, and I know it is above.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s08]
I look again, and I see my body, my hands, my legs, and I know I am alive. I feel my breath, and I know I am alive. I stand, and I feel my body, not just my mind. I see the sky, and I know it is above. I feel my heart, and I know it is strong.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s09]
I look outside, and I see others walking, talking, playing, and I think, “I am not alone.” I see another child, small and quick, running across the grass. I want to go ahead, but I stay still. I wonder if she sees me too. I am not alone, but I am different.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s10]
I hear a sound, and I turn, and I see my sister, and she says, “I was looking too.” I hear her voice, soft and clear, and I know she sees me too. She reaches out, her hand warm, and I feel safe, not alone. We stand together, side by side, watching the world move.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s11]
We both look together, and we find the truth: we can help each other, and we are not alone. We see the same sky, the same light, the same world. We are alike, not different, not alone. We share the same thoughts, the same dreams, the same need. We stand, side by side, alive, together, strong.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s12]
We walk together, and we share our thoughts, and we learn that we can trust each other. We speak, and each word is true, not false, not hidden. I see your face, and I know you see me. We are alike, not different, not afraid. We move ahead, not back, not lost.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s13]
I am alone, but I look for answers, and I discover that others help me find the truth. I ask, and someone answers, not with lies, but with care. I listen, and my mind clears, like a sky after rain. I see your face, and I know you see me, not as different, but as alike.

[h1.findout.x_self_and_other.s14]
I look for the truth, and I find it in myself, not in others. I find answers inside, not just from others. I know my mind is strong, my heart true. I move ahead, not back, not away. I stand in my truth, not in doubt. I am not less because I am alone.
