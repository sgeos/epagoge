[f1.danger.d00]
Some things hurt you. You cannot always see which. You have to be told.

[f1.danger.s01]
The boy was playing near the stove and reached for a hot pan.

[f1.danger.s02]
He did not know the pan was hot and touched it with his hand.

[f1.danger.s03]
His hand burned and he felt sharp pain.

[f1.danger.s04]
She showed him how to check if something is hot before touching it.

[f1.danger.s05]
He remembered to ask before touching anything new.

[f1.danger.s06]
He stayed safe by being careful and listening to others.
