[r1.written.v2.d00]
Make a mark so you know later. The mark stays after you forget.

[r1.written.v2.d01]
A copy is a thing made to look like another thing, exactly as it is.

[r1.written.v2.d02]
A diary is a book where you write notes about your day, every day.

[r1.written.v2.d03]
A list is a group of words or things written in order, one after another.
