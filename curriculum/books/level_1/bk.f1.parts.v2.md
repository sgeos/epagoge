[f1.parts.v2.d00]
A thing is made of parts. One bad part can stop the whole thing.

[f1.parts.v2.d01]
A part is a piece of something larger that helps it work.

[f1.parts.v2.d02]
A whole is all of something together, not broken or missing.

[f1.parts.v2.s01]
A small toy car is built from many parts.

[f1.parts.v2.s02]
Each part fits into place to make the whole car move.

[f1.parts.v2.s03]
One small part, a tiny wheel, is bent and cannot roll.

[f1.parts.v2.s04]
The bent wheel stops the whole car from going forward.

[f1.parts.v2.s05]
The car cannot go because one bad part breaks the whole.

[f1.parts.v2.s06]
The child sees the broken wheel and feels sad.

[f1.parts.v2.s07]
She waits for a new wheel to come and fix the whole.

[f1.parts.v2.s08]
Only when the part is fixed does the whole work again.

[f1.parts.v2.s09]
The car moves once more, whole and ready to play.

[f1.parts.v2.x12]
She places the car on the ground, gives it a gentle push, and watches it roll forward, smooth and strong, full of life once more.

[f1.parts.v2.x13]
The sun shines bright above as the car moves across the grass, full of life and ready to play again.

[f1.parts.v2.x14]

[f1.parts.v2.x15]
