Is the stove hot? The stove is hot because it has fire inside it.

[p1.heat.q1.a02]
What happens when you touch the stove? You can get burned because the stove is hot and fire can hurt you.

[p1.heat.q1.a03]
Is the water cold? The water is cold because it is not near the fire and does not have heat.

[p1.heat.q1.a04]
What happens when cold water is near heat? The cold water gets warm and may start to steam.

[p1.heat.q1.a05]
What happens when water gets very hot? The water melts into steam because heat changes it.

[p1.heat.q1.a06]
Can cold water freeze? Yes, cold water can freeze and become ice when it is cold enough.

[p1.heat.q1.a07]
What does cool mean? Cool means not hot, and it feels safe and calm, like the air after rain.

Is the stove hot? The stove is hot.

What happens when heat touches ice? The ice melts because of the heat.

Can you touch the stove without getting hurt? No, you cannot touch the stove without getting burned.
