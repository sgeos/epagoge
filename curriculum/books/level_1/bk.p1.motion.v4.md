[p1.motion.v4.d01]
To go up something with your hands and feet.

[p1.motion.v4.d02]
To pull something heavy along the ground.

[p1.motion.v4.d03]
To move slowly without force, like on water.

[p1.motion.v4.d04]
To stay on top of water or air without sinking.

[p1.motion.v4.d05]
To move through the air, like a bird.

[p1.motion.v4.d06]
A strong push or pull that makes something move.
