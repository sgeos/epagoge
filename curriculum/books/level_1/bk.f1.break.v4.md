[f1.break.v4.d00]
Things break. Broken is hard to undo. Things wear out from being used.

[f1.break.v4.d01]
A small break in something hard, like glass or ice.

[f1.break.v4.d02]
Piece means a small part of something larger that can be separated.

[f1.break.v4.d03]
Smash means to hit something hard and break it into many pieces.

[f1.break.v4.s01]
The old wooden bridge lasted many years, but one day it cracked under the weight of the cart.

[f1.break.v4.s02]
The boy tried to fix it but found only a piece of the broken wheel.

[f1.break.v4.s03]
He tried to glue the piece back, but the glue would not hold.

[f1.break.v4.s04]
The bridge was tired from years of use, and the wood had worn thin from rain and wind.

[f1.break.v4.s05]

[f1.break.v4.s06]

[f1.break.v4.s07]
He walked back to the village, tired and sore, with his shoes soaked and heavy.

[f1.break.v4.s08]
His mother saw him and said, “You should have stayed home,” but she still gave him a warm blanket.

[f1.break.v4.s09]
He knew he could not fix it again, and that things once broken never return to be whole.
