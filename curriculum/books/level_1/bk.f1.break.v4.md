[f1.break.v4.d00]
Things break. Broken is hard to undo. Things wear out from being used.

[f1.break.v4.d01]
A small break in something hard, like glass or ice.

[f1.break.v4.d02]
Piece means a small part of something larger that can be separated.

[f1.break.v4.d03]
Smash means to hit something hard and break it into many pieces.

[f1.break.v4.s01]
The old wooden bridge lasted many years, but one day it cracked under the weight of the cart. The bridge cracked again, and the cart fell through the gap. The boy stood still, afraid of what might come next. He looked above, where the sky turned dark and cold. He tried to lift a piece, but it was too heavy.

[f1.break.v4.s02]
The boy tried to fix it but found only a piece of the broken wheel. The boy looked at the broken wheel piece, its metal edge sharp and cold. He tried to fit it back into the cart, but it would not sit right. The hole where it belonged was too wide, and the metal bent.

[f1.break.v4.s03]
He tried to glue the piece back, but the glue would not hold. The metal edge stayed sharp and cold. He could not fix it, not even with care. The piece did not belong in the cart again. He looked at it, wondering what to do. No one else could help, not even his dad.

The bridge was tired from years of use, and the wood had worn thin from rain and wind. No one had walked across it in days, not even the children. The bridge was not alive, but it still felt the weight of the world. It had once carried carts, children, and even a dog.

His mother saw him and said, “You should have stayed home,” but she still gave him a warm blanket. He felt the weight of it, heavy like a secret. The air was cold, and his breath came in small clouds. He placed it on the ground, not to throw away, but to remember.

[f1.break.v4.s09]
He knew he could not fix it again, and that things once broken never return to be whole. He felt the weight of what was lost, deeper than the dirt below. The blanket stayed warm, but it could not mend what was broken. He knew he could not go back, not even to the start.
