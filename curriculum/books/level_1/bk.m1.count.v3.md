[m1.count.v3.d00]
One, two, three. Each thing gets the next number. The order never changes.

[m1.count.v3.d01]
Count means to say numbers in order to know how many there are.

[m1.count.v3.d02]
Eight is a number that comes after seven and before nine.

[m1.count.v3.d03]
Five is a number that comes after four and before six.

[m1.count.v3.d04]
Four is a number that comes after three and before five.

[m1.count.v3.d05]
Single means one thing, not more than one.

[m1.count.v3.d06]
Zero is a number that means no thing at all.

[m1.count.v3.s01]
One is the first number, and it comes before two.
