[f1.alive.v2.d00]
Living things die. Dying does not go backwards.

[f1.alive.v2.d01]
Alive means able to live, breathe, and move, not dead.

[f1.alive.v2.d02]
Asleep means resting with eyes closed and not awake.

[f1.alive.v2.d03]
Awake means not asleep, able to see, hear, and think.

[f1.alive.v2.d04]
A dead thing is not alive and cannot eat, drink, or move.

[f1.alive.v2.d05]
Death means the end of life, when a living thing stops living.

[f1.alive.v2.d06]
Die means to stop living, to become dead.

[f1.alive.v2.d07]
Drink means to take liquid into the mouth and swallow it.

[f1.alive.v2.s04]

[f1.alive.v2.s05]

[f1.alive.v2.s06]
