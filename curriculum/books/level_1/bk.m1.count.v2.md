[m1.count.v2.d00]
One, two, three. Each thing gets the next number. The order never changes.

[m1.count.v2.d01]
Count means to say numbers in order to find how many there are.

[m1.count.v2.d02]
Eight is a number that comes after seven and before nine.

[m1.count.v2.d03]
Five is a number that comes after four and before six.

[m1.count.v2.d04]
Four is a number that comes after three and before five.

[m1.count.v2.d05]
Pair means two things that are the same or belong together.

[m1.count.v2.d06]
A point is a small mark or the end of something sharp.

[m1.count.v2.s01]
One comes first, then two, then three, each thing gets the next number.

[m1.count.v2.s02]
Five is more than four, and four is more than three.

[m1.count.v2.s03]
A pair of shoes fits one foot each, and both are alike.

[m1.count.v2.s04]
A match is a small stick that can burn and make light.

[m1.count.v2.s05]
Zero means no apples in the basket.

[m1.count.v2.s06]
Eight is more than five, and five is more than four.

[m1.count.v2.s07]
The order of numbers is always the same.

[m1.count.v2.s08]
One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight.

[m1.count.v2.x15]
Eight is more than seven, and seven is more than six.
