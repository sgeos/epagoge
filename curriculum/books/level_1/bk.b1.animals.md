A dog is living and it moves. It has eyes to see with.

[b1.animals.d01]
An animal is a living thing that can move and feel.

[b1.animals.d02]
An arm is a part of the body that helps to hold or carry things.

[b1.animals.d03]
A body is the whole outside part of a living thing.

[b1.animals.d04]
An ear is a part of the body that helps to hear sounds.

[b1.animals.d05]
An eye is a part of the body that helps to see light and shapes.

[b1.animals.s01]
The dog woke up and looked around. It smelled the air and felt the breeze. The dog looked ahead and saw a bright blue ball. It moved slowly, then faster, across the grass. The dog reached it with its mouth. It pulled the ball back and held it. The dog felt strong and alive.

[b1.animals.s02]
The dog moved its body forward and tried to catch the bug. It shook its fur, then stood, eyes on the bug. The bug flew toward a tree, a black shape against the sky. The dog followed, moving fast along the path. It sniffed the air, felt the breeze, and kept going.

[b1.animals.s03]
The bug ran fast and jumped over a small block. The bug moved ahead, small and quick. It sniffed the breeze, ears forward. A blue butterfly flew above, wings bright. It wanted to catch, to follow, to stay. The dog stood still, listening, waiting. The world felt quiet, full of hidden things.

[b1.animals.s04]
The dog turned its head and used its ear to listen for the bug. The dog moved its body forward, legs strong and quick. The bug had gone near the blue bowl, a bright spot in the light. It did not care about the sky, the clouds, or the trees.

[b1.animals.s05]
The dog saw the bug near the blue bowl and ran with its legs. The dog ran across the grass, its body alive and moving fast. It felt the air rush past its face, strong and quick. The bug was small, but the dog was faster, able to follow its path.

[b1.animals.s06]
The dog caught the bug and held it in its mouth. It felt the sun above, bright and strong. The bug was gone, but the dog remembered. It moved its head, slow and careful. The air was full of small sounds. The grass bent near the blue bowl. It had caught the bug, and now it rested.

[b1.animals.s07]
The dog closed its eyes and slept under the warm sun. The sun moved above, bright and strong. The air held the smell of earth and flowers. A breeze touched its fur, soft and gentle. No one was near, no one to blame. It rested, safe and alone. The sky above was wide and clear.

[b1.animals.x13]
A bird flew above the blue bowl and landed on the edge. The dog stood, its body strong and ready. It moved along the path, slow at first. Then faster, following the sound of wings. The sky above was bright and wide. The bowl held water, clear and still. The bird looked down, eyes sharp and bright.

[b1.animals.x14]
The dog opened its eyes and sniffed the air, then stood up to follow the sound. It took one step forward, then another, eyes fixed on the sky. The air was warm, thick with the smell of dirt and flowers. It knew the way, even if no one else did.

[b1.animals.x15]
The sun shone bright and the air felt warm and still. The path ahead curved, hidden by trees. It followed the line of broken branches, moving slowly. It passed a red ball left in the dirt, forgotten. The dog stopped again, listening to the breeze. It took another step, then another, deeper into the clearing.
