[h1.faith.d00]
Someone may be watching. You cannot tell. Do your work as though.

[h1.faith.d01]
To accept something as true, even without proof.

[h1.faith.d12]
To trust someone or something to do the right thing.

[h1.faith.d15]
To trust someone or something to be good and honest.

[h1.faith.d16]
To trust someone or something without fear of harm.

[h1.faith.s01]
I wake up early, not because I must, but because I believe the world is watching.

[h1.faith.s02]
I build a small fire, not for heat, but to show I am alive and awake.

[h1.faith.s03]
I walk outside, not to go far, but to prove I can move without fear.

[h1.faith.s04]
I look at the sky, not to see, but to believe it is bright above me.

[h1.faith.s05]
I listen, not to sound, but to hear if my own thoughts are true.

[h1.faith.s06]
I sit by the tree, not to rest, but to show I still have faith.

[h1.faith.s07]
I open them again, not to see, but to know I am still here.

[h1.faith.s08]
I stand, not to run, but to stand tall as though someone sees.

[h1.faith.s09]
I go back inside, not to hide, but to keep doing my work, as though I am not alone.

[h1.faith.s13]
I put an apple on the table not to eat, but to show I am not afraid.

[h1.faith.s14]
I write a note just to prove I still trust my own thoughts.
