[h1.faith.d00]
Someone may be watching. You cannot tell. Do your work as though.

[h1.faith.d01]
To accept something as true, even without proof.

[h1.faith.d12]
To trust someone or something to do the right thing.

[h1.faith.d15]
To trust someone or something to be good and honest.

[h1.faith.d16]
To trust someone or something without fear of harm.

[h1.faith.s01]
I wake up early, not because I must, but because I believe the world is watching. I am awake, not because I must, but because I believe the world is watching. I do not know what is real, but I feel the weight of being seen. Even the trees stand still, their branches frozen in place.

[h1.faith.s02]
I build a small fire, not for heat, but to show I am alive and awake. No one answers, but I feel seen, even if no one is there. I whisper my name into the air, just to know I am still able to speak. I do not know if the wind carries my voice, or if it is lost in the dark.

[h1.faith.s03]
I walk outside, not to go far, but to prove I can move without fear. No one answers, but I feel watched, though no one is there. I raise my hand, not to wave, but to test if I can still move. I think about my sister, her voice, her laugh, her name.

[h1.faith.s04]
I look at the sky, not to see, but to believe it is bright above me. The air feels still, though I know it moves. I am not alone, though no one is near. I feel watched, not by a face, but by the space between trees. I stand, not to run, but to stay.

[h1.faith.s05]
I listen, not to sound, but to hear if my own thoughts are true. The air feels thick, as if it holds a secret I cannot name. I hear nothing, yet I feel watched, not by a face, but by the space between trees. The sky above is bright, but I wonder if it is real or just a trick of the light.

[h1.faith.s06]
I sit by the tree, not to rest, but to show I still have faith. The air is still, yet I feel watched, as if the space above holds a secret. I do not know who, or what, might be watching, but I stay awake. I try to believe I am alive, not just a shape in the light.

[h1.faith.s07]
I open them again, not to see, but to know I am still here. I am not alone, though no one appears. I hear a sound, like a breath, or a whisper. I try to believe I am alive, not just a shape in the light. I stay awake, though my body wants to sleep.

[h1.faith.s08]
I stand, not to run, but to stand tall as though someone sees. I feel the air move, like a breath above my skin. I am alive, not just a shape in the light. I hear a sound, like a leaf falling, or a whisper in the trees. I do not turn, though I feel eyes on my back.

[h1.faith.s09]
I go back inside, not to hide, but to keep doing my work, as though I am not alone. I stay awake, not to sleep, but to watch the light above the window. I feel the air move, like a hand on my back, but no one is there. I am not alone, even if I cannot see.

[h1.faith.s13]
I put an apple on the table not to eat, but to show I am not afraid. The light above the window stays bright, though no one is there. I hear a sound like a breath, but no one is near. The air moves again, like a hand across my back.

[h1.faith.s14]
I write a note just to prove I still trust my own thoughts. The air moves again, like a hand across my back. I am not afraid, though I feel watched. Something hides in the corner, behind the curtain. I check the window, but no one is above. The light stays bright, though no one is near.
