I trade my coin for a cup that belongs to her. I feel the cup is mine now, though it belongs to her. I cannot stop thinking about keeping it, even though I promised to return it. I am afraid she might be angry if I do not give it back.

She gives me her cup because she wants to swap with me. I feel the cup is mine now, though it belongs to her. I cannot stop thinking about keeping it, even though I promised to return it. I am afraid she might be angry if I do not give it back.

[ac1.having.x_exchange.s04]
I pay with my coin, and she says it is fair, not unfair. The coin is small but it holds meaning. It was not just a trade, but a promise. I accept the cup back, and she accepts my coin. We are alike in how we want things to be fair.

[ac1.having.x_exchange.s05]
Now I have two cups: one mine, one hers, but I keep them both. I am able to share because we both agree to care. We keep them together, not apart, not alone. The cups are alike in shape, but different in use. I feel safe with them near, not hidden away.

[ac1.having.x_exchange.s06]
We call it ours, not just mine or hers, because we made it together. We made it together, so it belongs to us both. It feels right to share because we both agree. We keep it safe, not hidden, not lost. We are able to trust because we are honest.

[ac1.having.x_exchange.s07]
I give her a gift, and she gives me a gift—this is how we trade. I trade my apple for her balloon, and we both feel full. We agree on the swap, and it feels right. We share because we trust, not because we must. The gift is not lost, it is passed, not taken.

[ac1.having.x_exchange.s08]
We say, “This belongs to us,” and we feel rich, not poor, even with no gold. We share the same sky, and the same air, and the same light. We build things together, and we care for each other, and we grow strong. We are not afraid to be different, because we are alike in heart.

[ac1.having.x_exchange.s11]
