[f1.break.v3.d00]
Things break. Broken is hard to undo. Things wear out from being used.

[f1.break.v3.d01]
Piece means a small part of something larger that has been separated.

[f1.break.v3.d02]
Smash means to hit something very hard so it breaks into many pieces.

[f1.break.v3.s01]
The old wooden chair had lasted many years, but one day it cracked under the weight. Pieces flew across the room, landing near the window. The child sat on the floor, eyes wide with fear. No one could fix it again. The wood was too weak, too old, too broken.

No one could fix it again. The wood was too weak, too old, too broken. The chair had once been strong, but now it was just a pile of broken wood.

The crack ran deep, like a line drawn in the dirt. He thought about asking his dad, but the man was asleep in the bed.
