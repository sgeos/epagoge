She said the cup was full. Write down that it was her who said it.

[r1.who.v4.d01]
A mark is a sign made by writing or drawing.

[r1.who.v4.s01]
She said the cup was full. The cup had a mark on its side. The mark was a circle, bright and round. It looked like a sun or a small moon. The mark was made by a sharp stone. The stone was smooth and black. The cup was made of clay, not metal.

[r1.who.v4.s02]
The cup had a mark on its side. The mark was above the liquid line. The mark showed where the water stopped. The mark was round like a sun or a moon. The mark was made by a sharp stone. The stone was smooth and black. The cup was made of clay, not metal.

[r1.who.v4.s03]
She said the cup was full, and the mark showed where the liquid reached. The liquid was clear, like a stream after rain. It stayed above the water, as if it knew the truth. The hands were hers, though she was not there. The cup held water, not air, not stone, not fire.

[r1.who.v4.s04]
Her name was on the cup, so it belonged to her. The cup was full of water, and she had filled it herself. She had been careful, not to spill a drop. She had come back to the cup after a long time. She had not been there, but she knew the cup.
