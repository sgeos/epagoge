[h1.faith.q1.d00]
Someone may be watching. You cannot tell. Do your work as though.

[h1.faith.q1.d01]
To think something is true, even if you cannot see it.

[h1.faith.q1.d02]
A strong trust in something or someone, even without proof.

[h1.faith.q1.d03]
To believe someone or something is good, safe, or honest.

[h1.faith.q1.a01]
Can you see the wind? No, you cannot see the wind, but you can feel it blowing.

[h1.faith.q1.a02]
Can you see your breath in cold air? No, you cannot see your breath, but you can feel it when you breathe out.

[h1.faith.q1.a03]
Can you see a promise? No, you cannot see a promise, but you can keep it.

[h1.faith.q1.a04]
Can you see the sun at night? No, you cannot see the sun at night, but you can trust it will rise again.

[h1.faith.q1.a05]
Can you see faith? No, you cannot see faith, but you can believe in it.

[h1.faith.q1.a06]
Can you see a secret? No, you cannot see a secret, but you can keep it.

[h1.faith.q1.a07]
Can you see what someone is thinking? No, you cannot see what someone is thinking, but you can listen and believe them.

[h1.faith.q1.a08]
Can you trust yourself when no one else is around? Yes, you can trust yourself when no one else is around, because faith in yourself means you believe in doing the right thing, even when you are alone.

[h1.faith.q1.a09]
Can you believe in something you cannot see? Yes, you can believe in things like air, even though you cannot see them.

[h1.faith.q1.a10]
Can you show faith when no one is watching? Yes, you can show faith by doing the right thing, even when no one sees.

[h1.faith.q1.a11]
What happens if you do your work as though someone is watching? You do your best, even if no one is there to see.

[h1.faith.q1.a12]
Does trust mean you must always agree? No, trust means you believe someone is trying their best, even if you disagree sometimes.
