There is enough water for one plant. There is not enough for two.

[a1.enough.d01]
Extra water is what remains after the need is met, but there is no extra here.

[a1.enough.d02]
There is no plenty of water, only just enough for one.

[a1.enough.d03]
A shortage of water means there is not enough for two plants to live.

[a1.enough.d04]
There is no spare water, so no second plant can grow.
