[p1.motion.v3.d01]
To go up something using your hands and feet.

[p1.motion.v3.d02]
To pull something heavy along the ground.

[p1.motion.v3.d03]
To stay on top of water or air without sinking.

[p1.motion.v3.d04]
A strong push or pull that makes something move.

[p1.motion.v3.s01]
It sits still. It is not afraid to stay. No one comes to move it. It remains on the ground. Its shape is clear and whole. It does not change or grow. No wind can lift it. No hand can take it. It is not broken or cracked. It stays in place.
