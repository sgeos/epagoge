[p1.heat.v3.d00]
The stove is hot. The water is cold.

[p1.heat.v3.d01]
A burn is when heat hurts skin, like touching a hot stove.

[p1.heat.v3.d02]
Cold is a feeling when the air or water is not warm, like ice water.

[p1.heat.v3.d03]
Cool means not hot, like a breeze on a warm day.

[p1.heat.v3.d04]
Freeze means to become solid when cold, like water turning to ice.

[p1.heat.v3.d05]
Melt means to turn from solid to liquid when hot, like ice in sun.
