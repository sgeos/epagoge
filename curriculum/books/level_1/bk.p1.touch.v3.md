[p1.touch.v3.d00]
Your hand is on the cup. Nothing moves until something touches it.

[p1.touch.v3.d01]
Contact means when one thing meets another without breaking apart.

[p1.touch.v3.d02]
Patted means to touch something gently with the hand, like a soft tap.

[p1.touch.v3.d03]
Pressed means to push something down with force, often using the hand.

[p1.touch.v3.d04]
Rub means to move one surface against another, often to clean or smooth.

[p1.touch.v3.d05]
Stroke means to move a hand gently over a surface or body part.

[p1.touch.v3.d06]
Touch means to come into contact with something, using a hand or part of the body.
