This book shows how words like hello, please, thank you, sorry, goodbye, and the words space, gap, hole, fill, full, empty, half, clear, and more help us live well together.

Hello, please, thank you, I am sorry, goodbye, and the words clear, empty, fill, full, gap, half, hole, space show how we care.

[i1.manners.x_emptiness.s05]
