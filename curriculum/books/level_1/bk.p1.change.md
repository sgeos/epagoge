[p1.change.d00]
Let go and it falls. Open it and it is open.

[p1.change.d01]
To change into something different is to become.

[p1.change.d02]
To make something different is to change.

[p1.change.d03]
To shut something so it is not open is to close.

[p1.change.d04]
To put something over something else is to cover.

[p1.change.d05]
To let something fall from your hand is to drop.

[p1.change.d06]
To move down from a higher place to a lower place is to fall.

[p1.change.d07]
To come down to the ground after falling is to land.

[p1.change.d24]
To grow over time is to become bigger.
