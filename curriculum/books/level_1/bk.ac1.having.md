[ac1.having.d00]
The cup is mine. That one is hers. Now I have two.

[ac1.having.d01]
To be owned by someone or something, like a cup that belongs to a person.

[ac1.having.d02]
Used to show something belongs to a girl or woman, like a cup that is hers.

[ac1.having.d03]
Used to show something belongs to a person speaking, like a cup that is mine.

[ac1.having.d04]
Used to show something belongs to more than one person, like a cup that is ours.

[ac1.having.d05]
To own something is to have it as your very own.

[ac1.having.s01]
The cup is mine because I made it with clay and baked it in the oven.

[ac1.having.s02]
That one is hers because she gave it to me as a gift when I was sad.

[ac1.having.s03]
I keep my cup on the shelf because it belongs to me and I like it best.

[ac1.having.s04]
We are not poor because we have two cups, and we share them with others.

[ac1.having.s05]
I am not rich, but I am happy with my own cup and hers.

[ac1.having.s06]
I will not steal hers because that would be wrong and I want to be honest.

[ac1.having.s07]
My sister says I am clever because I found two cups and know which is mine.

[ac1.having.x13]
I showed my sister the cup I made, and she smiled because it was bright and strong.

[ac1.having.x14]
I placed my cup next to hers on the shelf, and we both smiled.

[ac1.having.x15]
I placed my cup beside hers on the shelf, side by side, like two friends sharing the same space.
