[ac1.having.d00]
The cup is mine. That one is hers. Now I have two.

[ac1.having.d01]
To be owned by someone or something, like a cup that belongs to a person.

[ac1.having.d02]
Used to show something belongs to a girl or woman, like a cup that is hers.

[ac1.having.d03]
Used to show something belongs to a person speaking, like a cup that is mine.

[ac1.having.d04]
Used to show something belongs to more than one person, like a cup that is ours.

[ac1.having.d05]
To own something is to have it as your very own.

[ac1.having.s01]
The cup is mine because I made it with clay and baked it in the oven. It feels warm and smooth, like my own skin. No one else has a cup like this one. It is mine, and mine alone. I will keep it safe, even when I grow old.

[ac1.having.s02]
That one is hers because she gave it to me as a gift when I was sad. It feels warm and smooth, like my own skin. No one else has a cup like this one. It is mine, and mine alone. I will keep it safe, even when I grow old.

[ac1.having.s03]
I keep my cup on the shelf because it belongs to me and I like it best. It sits above the others, bright and clean, like a star in a sky of glass. No one else can touch it, not even my brother, not even my aunt. The cup holds more than water—it holds my name, my breath, my life.

[ac1.having.s04]
We are not poor because we have two cups, and we share them with others. I can share my cup with anyone who asks, even if they are not my own. The cups are alike, though one is bigger, and both hold water. I am able to pass one to my sister when she is thirsty.

[ac1.having.s05]
I am not rich, but I am happy with my own cup and hers. It is not bigger, but it holds water just the same. I can share it, but I will not take it from her. She is not afraid, and I am not angry. I know what is right, and I will not cheat.

[ac1.having.s06]
I will not steal hers because that would be wrong and I want to be honest. I know my cup is mine and I will not take hers. I can share the water but not the cup itself. I am not afraid to say no, even when others want to take.

[ac1.having.s07]
My sister says I am clever because I found two cups and know which is mine. I know my cup is mine and I will not take hers. I can share the water but not the cup itself. I am not afraid to say no, even when others want to take.

[ac1.having.x13]
I showed my sister the cup I made, and she smiled because it was bright and strong. It is bright like the sun and strong like a rock. I know my cup is special because I built it myself. It feels warm in my hand, like a friend. I keep it near my bed, where it belongs.

[ac1.having.x14]
I placed my cup next to hers on the shelf, and we both smiled. The light above us shone bright and warm, like the sun. I felt safe, alive, and strong, like a rock. We sat together, side by side, not apart. No one could take my cup, not even a thief.

[ac1.having.x15]
