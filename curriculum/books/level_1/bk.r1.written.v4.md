[r1.written.v4.d00]
Make a mark so you know later. The mark stays after you forget.

[r1.written.v4.d01]
A copy is a copy made by repeating something exactly.

[r1.written.v4.d02]
A diary is a book where you write notes about your life.

[r1.written.v4.d03]
A list is a group of things written down in order.

[r1.written.v4.s01]
I made a mark on the wall with a stick because I wanted to remember where I hid my toy. The mark stayed even after I forgot the day I hid it. I knew the mark was real because it was deep and sharp. I touched it again to feel the shape.

[r1.written.v4.s02]
The mark stayed even after I forgot the day I hid it. It was still sharp and deep, like it knew I would come back. I touched it again to feel the shape, just to be sure. The shape was clear, like a line drawn by a strong hand. I remembered the stick, the way it broke a little when I pressed.

[r1.written.v4.s03]
I found the mark again and knew where the toy was. The mark was deep, like it had been carved with care. I followed the line across the grass to the old tree. I pulled it out and held it close, warm and alive. I did not know why I had chosen that spot.

[r1.written.v4.s04]
I kept the note in my pocket so I would not lose it. The paper felt smooth, like a leaf from a tree. The mark was still clear, drawn with care. I thought about the place where the toy had been. The grass was dry, the air still. I felt the weight of the note in my hand.

[r1.written.v4.s05]
I looked at the note every morning to remember. The mark was still clear, drawn with care. I felt the weight of the note in my hand. The grass was dry, the air still. I thought about the place where the toy had been. The paper felt smooth, like a leaf from a tree.

[r1.written.v4.s06]
The log helped me remember what I did and where I left things. I marked the spot with a stick, sharp and straight. The mark was clear, made with care, like a line drawn in stone. No one else could see it, but I could. I felt calm, like the air above the hill.

[r1.written.v4.s07]
I used a list to check if I had all my toys. Each toy had a place, and I marked it with a line. The list helped me see what was missing, and what I had. I checked again, and again, to be sure. No toy was left behind, no toy lost.

[r1.written.v4.s08]
The list stayed clean and neat, even when I was tired. I kept my mark above the line, just so I would know. No one else could read it, but I could. The mark was small, but it was mine. It showed I had been there, and I had tried.

[r1.written.v4.s09]
I made a diary to write about my day. I write when the light is bright and the air is still. My hand moves slow, but the mark stays true. I use a pen that feels like a friend. The paper is clean, like a path through the forest. The diary keeps a record of my breath, my thoughts, my care.

[r1.written.v4.s10]
The diary told me what I did, even when I forgot. The diary showed me the apple I left on the table, though I thought it gone. I saw the mark I made above the page, clear as a path through the forest. The pen still felt like a friend, though I had not held it for days.

[r1.written.v4.s11]
I read the diary again and remembered the good things. The mark showed me the apple I had left, still there, though I thought it gone. I saw the note I wrote, small but true, about the day I felt alive. The log, the record, the diary, all stayed, even when I forgot.

[r1.written.v4.s12]
The mark, the note, the list, the log, the record, and the diary all stayed, even when I forgot. I made a mark so I would know later. I remembered the day I felt alive, not afraid, not alone. The list helped me find my way, step by step, through the forest.
