[r1.written.v4.d00]
Make a mark so you know later. The mark stays after you forget.

[r1.written.v4.d01]
A copy is a copy made by repeating something exactly.

[r1.written.v4.d02]
A diary is a book where you write notes about your life.

[r1.written.v4.d03]
A list is a group of things written down in order.

[r1.written.v4.s01]
I made a mark on the wall with a stick because I wanted to remember where I hid my toy.

[r1.written.v4.s02]
The mark stayed even after I forgot the day I hid it.

[r1.written.v4.s03]
I found the mark again and knew where the toy was.

[r1.written.v4.s04]
I kept the note in my pocket so I would not lose it.

[r1.written.v4.s05]
I looked at the note every morning to remember.

[r1.written.v4.s06]
The log helped me remember what I did and where I left things.

[r1.written.v4.s07]
I used a list to check if I had all my toys.

[r1.written.v4.s08]
The list stayed clean and neat, even when I was tired.

[r1.written.v4.s09]
I made a diary to write about my day.

[r1.written.v4.s10]
The diary told me what I did, even when I forgot.

[r1.written.v4.s11]
I read the diary again and remembered the good things.

[r1.written.v4.s12]
The mark, the note, the list, the log, the record, and the diary all stayed, even when I forgot.
