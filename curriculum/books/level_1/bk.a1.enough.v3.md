[a1.enough.v3.d00]
There is enough water for one plant. There is not enough for two.

[a1.enough.v3.d01]
Enough means having as much as needed, not more and not less.

[a1.enough.v3.d02]
A container is filled when it holds all the liquid it can hold.

[a1.enough.v3.d03]
Lack means not having enough of something that is needed.

[a1.enough.v3.d04]
Plenty means there is more than enough of something.

[a1.enough.v3.d05]
Shortage means not having enough of something when it is needed.
