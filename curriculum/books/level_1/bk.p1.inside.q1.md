[p1.inside.q1.a01]
Where is the water now? The water is outside the cup. It flows across the table. The cup is empty and still. The water moves slowly toward the edge. It slips down and drops into the bowl. The bowl is blue and round. The water fills the bowl. The bowl is not full yet.

[p1.inside.q1.a02]
Is the water inside the cup? No, the water is not inside the cup. The water is in the bowl. It stays still and blue. The bowl holds the water now. No more flows from the cup. The cup is empty and dry. The table is clean and flat. The water does not move again.

[p1.inside.q1.a03]
Where was the water before it came out? The water was inside the cup before it came out. It stayed still and blue inside the cup. The cup held the water above the table. The water did not move again. The cup was empty and dry. The table is clean and flat.

[p1.inside.q1.a04]
Can the water fit in the cup? Yes, the water can fit in the cup. The water is blue and still. It stays inside the cup above the table. The cup holds the water without spilling. The water does not move again. The cup is full and not empty. The table is clean and flat.

[p1.inside.q1.a05]
Did the water enter the cup? Yes, the water entered the cup. The water is in the cup. It stays above the table. The cup holds the water without spilling. The water is blue and still. It does not move again. The cup is full and not empty. The table is clean and flat.

[p1.inside.q1.a06]
What happens when the water leaves the cup? The water leaves the cup and goes outside. The water leaves the cup and flows across the floor. It moves away from the table and down the path. The water is no longer above the table. It goes outside where the sky is bright and blue.

[p1.inside.q1.a07]
Is the cup still a container? Yes, the cup is still a container. The cup is still a container. It can hold things again. The water is not inside it now. The cup is empty and alone. It sits on the table, still able to hold. The cup is not broken, not gone.

[p1.inside.q1.a08]
Where is the water now that it is out? The water is outside the cup. It lies on the table, still alive and moving. The cup is empty, still able to hold. The water is not inside, not gone. It flows across the surface, slow and clear. The air above is warm, not cold.

[p1.inside.q1.a09]
Can the water go back into the cup? Yes, the water can go back into the cup. The water is inside the cup again. The cup is full, no longer empty, no longer able to hold more. The water flows not across the table, but inside the cup, safe and whole.

[p1.inside.q1.a15]
Is the water inside the cup? The water is inside the cup. The cup is full, not empty, not broken, not lost. The water stays, does not drip, does not flow away. The cup holds the water, safe and whole. The water is not above, not below, not across the table.
