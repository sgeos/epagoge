[p1.join.v3.d00]
Put the lid on the pot. Take the lid off the pot.

[p1.join.v3.d01]
Separated by space or distance.

[p1.join.v3.d02]
Separate means to pull things apart.

[p1.join.v3.s01]
The lid is apart from the pot. The pot sits on the stove, warm and still. The lid lies on the table, cool and quiet. I pick up the lid, its edge sharp and round. The lid is made of metal, bright and smooth. It has a handle, long and curved.

[p1.join.v3.s02]
I use glue to join the lid to the pot. I try to join the lid to the pot with glue. I press it down, hard, with both hands. The glue holds, like a promise. The pot is warm, the lid is cool. I wait, still, quiet, breathing slow. The lid is now joined, no more apart.

[p1.join.v3.s03]
The lid is now joined to the pot. It stays in place, no longer apart. I cannot lift it, not even a little. The glue is strong, like a promise kept. The pot is warm, the lid is cool. I wait, still, quiet, breathing slow. The air is thick, the light is bright.

[p1.join.v3.s04]
I tie the lid to the pot with a string. I pull it once, then again, to make sure it stays. I cannot lift it, not even a little. The string holds, like a promise kept. The air is quiet, the light is bright. I wait, still, breathing slow. The lid is fixed, not moving, not lost.

[p1.join.v3.s06]
I undo the string to separate the lid. The lid lifts, slow and clear, above the pot. Air flows through the gap, warm and bright. I hold it, still, not afraid, not lost. The lid is not tied, not stuck, not dead. It is alive, separate, able to move. The metal is cool, smooth, not sharp.

[p1.join.v3.s07]
The lid is now separate from the pot. It rests on the table, still cool and smooth. I place it above the pot, ready to fit. The metal is alike in shape, but not the same. I know it belongs here, not alone. I lift it slowly, aiming to fit it back.

[p1.join.v3.s09]
