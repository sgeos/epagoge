[i1.reason.x_emptiness.d00]
This book shows why a gap is not full when space is empty, and why filling it makes it full again.

[i1.reason.x_emptiness.s01]
A child finds a hole in the ground and tries to fill it with dirt, but the hole stays empty because the dirt keeps slipping through. A child sits beside the hole and feels the air above it, cold and still. The dirt slips through like a secret no one can keep.

[i1.reason.x_emptiness.s02]
The hole is not full because the space inside is still empty. Air moves above it, cold and still, like a breath held too long. It does not answer, but it listens. It is a place where something waits to be named. The gap is not empty because it holds nothing.

[i1.reason.x_emptiness.s03]
The child tries again, but the dirt slips away, leaving the gap still empty. The child sits alone, breathing slow, eyes on the gap that does not fill. Air moves above, still as a held thought, cold and deep. The space is not empty because it waits to be named, to be known.

[i1.reason.x_emptiness.s04]
The child then finds a bucket and tries to fill the hole with water, but the water drains out, showing the space is still empty. The child watches the water drain, slow and sure, as if the hole remembers what it is to be full. The air above feels heavier, thick with the weight of things not said.

[i1.reason.x_emptiness.s05]
The child explains, “The hole is empty because the space below is open and no block can stop the flow.” The child feels the air above, thick with what is not said. The hole remains open, waiting for a reason that will not come. The child does not cry, but the eyes are dry, like stone.

[i1.reason.x_emptiness.s06]
The child builds a wall of blocks around the hole, and now the space holds water. The child watches the water rise, clear and still, above the hole. No sound comes from below, only the air, thick with what is not said. The wall holds, built strong, block by block, each one a promise.

That is not true because the hole is not full of water, even though it is near a stream. That is so because the hole is empty, not filled with water, even though water flows nearby. Air moves above the gap, thin and clear, like a breath held too long.
