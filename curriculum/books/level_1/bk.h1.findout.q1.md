[h1.findout.q1.d00]
How do you know? Let us go and find out. Now we have settled it.

[h1.findout.q1.d01]
To choose something after thinking about it.

[h1.findout.q1.d02]
To find something new or learn something for the first time.

[h1.findout.q1.d03]
Trying to see or find something.

[h1.findout.q1.d04]
Something that shows something is true.

[h1.findout.q1.d05]
To show something is true with proof.

[h1.findout.q1.a01]
How do you know if something is true? You must decide what you believe.

[h1.findout.q1.a02]
How can you find out if something is true? You must discover the answer by looking.

[h1.findout.q1.a03]
What do you do when you are looking for truth? You look for proof that shows the answer.

[h1.findout.q1.a04]
How do you prove something is true? You show proof that makes the truth clear.

[h1.findout.q1.a05]
What happens when you prove something? You can settle the question and stop arguing.

[h1.findout.q1.a06]
How do you know when you have settled it? You feel sure because you have proof.

[h1.findout.q1.a07]
What do you do when you have proof and have settled it? You share your answer with others.

[h1.findout.q1.a08]
How do you feel when you know the truth and have settled it? You feel safe, calm, and ready to move ahead.

[h1.findout.q1.a09]
How do you know what is real? You discover it by looking and learning.

[h1.findout.q1.a15]
How do you know what to do? You must decide what is best for you.
