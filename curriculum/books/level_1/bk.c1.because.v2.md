A ball rolled down a hill and fell because you let go.

[c1.because.v2.s02]

[c1.because.v2.s03]
It rolled into a gap and stopped, but you did not pick it up.

[c1.because.v2.s04]
You looked away, and it stayed there, alone and still.

[c1.because.v2.s05]
You thought it was lost, but you remembered the way back.

[c1.because.v2.s06]
You picked it up and held it close, feeling its round shape.

[c1.because.v2.s07]
You brought it back to the yard and placed it on a shelf.

[c1.because.v2.s08]
It was not broken, and you felt glad it was safe.

[c1.because.v2.s09]
You learned that letting go means the thing may not return.

[c1.because.v2.s10]
So you hold tight, and keep it near, so it will not fall again.
