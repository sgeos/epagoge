[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.d00]
Living things need food and water to keep going. They grow.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s01]
A baby bird cannot fly because it is too small and weak. It feels afraid of falling from the branch above. It wants to grow, but it cannot do it alone. The mother bird brings seeds and water every day. The baby bird drinks the water and eats the food.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s02]
The bird tries to grow strong by eating seeds and drinking water. It wants to fly, but its body is still too small. Each day, the mother bird brings more food. The baby bird eats, drinks, and rests. It feels the sun warm on its back. It dreams of flying across the sky.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s03]
The bird feels weak when it cannot find food, so it shrinks in size. It lies alone on the branch, cold and still. The sky above looks dark, the wind blows hard. It cannot move, cannot cry, cannot grow. Its body is small, smaller than before. The sun does not warm it now.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s04]
It grows again after the mother brings food and a warm blanket. The bird feels warm and safe, no longer afraid. The sun shines bright above, the sky clears. The breeze lifts its feathers, making it feel alive. It blinks, then sits up, its body stronger than before. It looks around, seeing the world in a new way.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s05]
The bird cannot stay still because it needs to learn to fly. It feels the air rush past, strong and alive. The breeze lifts it higher, like a balloon above the trees. It tries again, pushing off the branch with its small feet. The sky seems big, too big, but it must go ahead.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s06]
It grows stronger each day because it keeps trying to climb. It climbs higher, each step a little more sure. The sun warms its back, making it feel safe. It wants to fly, to go ahead, to reach the sky. It knows it must keep going, even when tired. The path ahead is long, but it is its own.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s07]
It shrinks when it is cold and has no food to eat. It hides under a rock, trying to stay warm. Its breath is slow, its eyes half closed. It cannot move, cannot think, cannot grow. The sky is gray and heavy, full of rain. It dreams of sun, of green leaves, of warm air.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s08]
It grows again because the sun comes out and the sky is bright. The ground feels warm after the cold. Its breath comes faster, stronger. It moves an arm, then a leg, slowly. The sky is blue, not gray. It lifts its head, looks above. A bird sings, high in the tree.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s09]
It needs food and water to grow strong. The sun helps the seed grow into a plant. The plant needs air and light to stay alive. The leaves open to catch the bright light. Each day it becomes bigger and stronger. It does not need to be afraid. It can grow even when the wind blows hard.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s10]
When it is too weak to stand, it shrinks in fear. It shrinks in fear because it feels weak and alone. The wind blows harder, and the plant cannot stand. It cannot grow when it is afraid. The sun hides behind a cloudy sky. No light comes to help it stay alive.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s11]
But when it gets food again, it grows stronger. The sun breaks through the clouds, warm and bright above. The plant feels safe, no longer afraid, and begins to grow faster. Each leaf opens wider, catching more air and light. It no longer fears the wind, for it knows it can stand.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s12]
It learns to fly because it needs to find more food. The wind lifts it higher, above the trees and the tall grass. It sees the sun, bright and warm, above the clouds. It feels the air rush past, strong and alive. It knows it must fly ahead, not stay behind.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s13]
The bird grows, shrinks, and grows again because it must live. The bird grows bigger because it needs to fly higher. Each breath helps it stay alive and strong. The wind pushes it forward, not back. It learns to follow the path through the trees. It sees a bright apple fall from a branch.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s14]
It stays alive because it keeps eating and drinking. It climbs a tree to find more food above. The wind helps it rise, not push it down. It follows the path through the forest, not back. It sees a bright light from a metal cup near a stream. The cup holds water, cool and clear, to drink.

[b1.keeping_going.x_cause_and_effect.s15]
It feels cold and tired, and its wings shrink from not moving. The wind lifts it again, helping it rise above the trees. The wind blows again, not pushing it down, but lifting it higher. It sees a path across the stream, not a gap or a fall. The branches bend, not break, as it climbs to reach the sky.
