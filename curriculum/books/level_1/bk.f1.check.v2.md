[f1.check.v2.d00]
Someone told you. Telling can be wrong. Go and look.

[f1.check.v2.d01]
Lie means to say something false, even when you know it is not true.

[f1.check.v2.d02]
Test means to try something to see if it works or is true.

[f1.check.v2.s01]
I saw a red apple on the table and knew it was real. It looked alive, bright, and real, like it belonged there. The air felt thick, heavy, like something was wrong. The apple seemed to watch me, its surface smooth and shiny. The room grew quiet, too quiet, like time had stopped.

[f1.check.v2.s02]
I tried to pick it up, but it felt heavy and wrong. The air above the table grew thick, like a blanket of fear. I could not move, not even to breathe. My hands shook, though I was not cold. The apple stayed still, its surface still bright. I did not believe it was real, yet it seemed alive.

[f1.check.v2.s03]
I checked the apple again and saw a small lie in its shape. I could not tell if it was real or a trick of the light. The air above it felt thicker than before, like a breath held too long. I tried to look away, but my eyes stayed fixed.

[f1.check.v2.s04]
I stared at it, and my mind grew clever with doubt. I reached out, not to touch, but to test the air around it. It felt warm, like a hand I had known before. Could this be a trick? A lie made real? I did not know, but I had to see.

[f1.check.v2.s05]
I knew it was not real, but I had to test it. It felt warm, like a breath I had once known. I thought of my mother, her voice soft, her eyes kind. I wondered if this was a dream, or a sign. I thought of the apple on the table, still whole, still red.

[f1.check.v2.s06]
I felt afraid and wanted to run away. The air grew thick, like a weight above my head. I could not move, not even to breathe. I thought of my mother, but she was not there. I heard a whisper, soft as a breeze. It said my name, but not my real name.

[f1.check.v2.s07]
I knew then that someone had lied to me. I remembered the apple, the one I thought was mine. It was not. It was stolen, just like my name. I saw the lie in the way the light bent above the table. The room was too still, too full of things not said.

[f1.check.v2.s08]
I went outside and looked at the sky. The sky was bright, full of light above the trees. I felt small, alone, yet alive in the open air. A balloon floated high, shaped like an apple, red and round. I wondered if it was real or just a dream. I reached up, but my hand could not touch it.

[f1.check.v2.s09]
I found a balloon that looked just like the apple. It floated above the grass, bright red and round. I reached up, but my hand could not touch it. I felt a pull, like something wanted me to go ahead. I stepped forward, careful not to break the air. The balloon seemed to know me, like an old friend.

[f1.check.v2.s10]
I learned to check things before I believe them. I looked again at the balloon, now still above the grass. It was not an apple, but a red ball, bright and round. The air felt heavy, like something was wrong. I saw a shadow move behind the trees. I did not go ahead, not this time.

[f1.check.v2.s11]
I went home and told my mother the truth. I told her about the balloon, how it looked like an apple. I believed her, even though I was still afraid. I just felt, deep inside, that I was alive and seen. I knew the truth was real, not a dream.
