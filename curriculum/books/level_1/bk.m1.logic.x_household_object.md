If it rains then the ground is wet. All, some, none. It cannot be both so and not so.

[m1.logic.x_household_object.s01]
A bag, basket, bed, bell, blanket, blocks, boat, bottle all sit in a yard when rain comes and the ground gets wet. A bag is not both a basket and a boat. The blanket lies above the blocks. A bottle cannot be both empty and full. The bed is not alike the boat.

[m1.logic.x_household_object.s02]
Rain falls and the ground is wet, so the boat floats and the bottle rolls. The boat floats above the water. The bottle rolls across the ground. A child sees the ball and picks it up. The blanket stays dry inside the bag. The blocks are not alike the boat.

[m1.logic.x_household_object.s03]
All the things on the ground are wet, but the blanket stays dry inside the bag. The bottle rolls across the floor, but the ball stays still. The blocks are not alike, and the boat floats above the water. The child cannot lift the bucket, but the dog pulls the rope.

[m1.logic.x_household_object.s04]
Some things are wet, but none are dry under the roof. The apple lies still, but the ball rolls across the floor. The blocks are not alike, and the boat floats above the water. The child cannot lift the bucket, but the dog pulls the rope. The blanket stays dry, and the blocks stay dry.

[m1.logic.x_household_object.s05]
It cannot be both so and not so, so the blanket stays dry and the blocks stay dry. The ball rolls across the floor, not above the table. The apple lies still, not alive in the basket. The boat floats above the water, not below the surface. The blocks are alike, not different in size or shape.

[m1.logic.x_household_object.s06]
Either the boat floats or it sinks, but not both at once. Either the boat floats above the water or it sinks below the surface, but not both at once. The blocks are alike in size and shape, not different in any way. The ball rolls across the floor, not above the table or below the rug.

The bell rings, and the blocks fall, but the bottle stays still. The bottle stays still, not moving above the floor or below the rug. The bell rings again, but the bottle does not answer. The air is still, not hot or cold, not dry or wet. The bottle is not afraid, not angry, not alone.

[m1.logic.x_household_object.s08]
The bed is dry, the basket is full, and the boat drifts on the wet ground. The boat floats, not sinking, not rising, just drifting above the floor. The basket holds apples, not stones, not toys, not anything broken. The bed is not soft, not hard, not warm, not cold, just dry.

[m1.logic.x_household_object.s09]
