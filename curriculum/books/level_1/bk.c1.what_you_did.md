[c1.what_you_did.s04]
I thought about hiding the pieces. I could not pretend they were still whole. I felt the weight of each broken part. I thought about the apple tree, the one we planted. I knew I could not lie, not even to the sky. I heard the wind, soft and low, like a whisper.

[c1.what_you_did.s09]
I could not keep the secret. I had to show what happened. I saw the ball on the ground, broken. I knew I had to admit it. I picked up the pieces. I walked slowly, not fast. I looked around, no one was near. I thought about asking for help.

[c1.what_you_did.s10]
I had to show what happened. I knew I was wrong. I felt afraid. I did not want to be a cheater. I had to admit it. I broke the ball. It was not anyone else. I did not blame the wind. I did not blame the tree. I did not blame the dog.

[c1.what_you_did.s11]
I did not try to blame anyone else. I knew I had to face it. I felt the truth inside. I could not hide it. I stood still and spoke. I said I broke the ball. It was not a lie. It was not a game. I had to accept it.

[c1.what_you_did.s12]
I did not run away. I stood still and faced the truth. I knew I had to accept what I did. The ball was broken, and I was the one who caused it. I could not hide from the feeling inside. I did not blame the wind, the floor, or the wall.
