[ac1.using_up.d00]
There was water. You drank some. Now there is less.

[ac1.using_up.d01]
To use means to take something to do a job or make something work.

[ac1.using_up.d132]
To spend means to use up something like time, and once used, it is gone.

[ac1.using_up.s01]
There was water in the bottle. The water felt cool and alive. The bottle was made of glass, smooth and clear. I could see the light through it, bright and sharp. The water had no shape of its own, but it filled the space. It rose and fell with the air, breathing slowly.

[ac1.using_up.x04]
The bottle felt lighter. The bottle felt lighter because the water was gone. I looked inside and saw only air. The glass was clear, but now empty. The air above the bottle was still. The bottle had no shape without the liquid. It seemed smaller, alone. I held it, but it gave no weight.

[ac1.using_up.x05]
Water dripped onto the floor. The floor got wet. A small puddle grew near the bottle. The drop came from above. It fell through the air. The sound was soft, like a whisper. The water was cold, but not ice. It moved slowly, not fast. The floor was made of wood.

[ac1.using_up.x06]
The floor got wet. Water spread across the wood like a secret. The puddle grew bigger, slow and quiet. A drop fell again from above, small and cold. No one came to stop it, no one to care. The water moved, not fast, not slow, just alive. It touched the edge of the basket, then the corner of the book.

[ac1.using_up.x07]
You tried to clean it with a cloth. The cloth soaked up the water, heavy and cold. You pulled it from the floor, dripping, slow. The water spread again, like a secret kept. The puddle grew, alive and quiet. You sat, back against the wall, tired. You thought of the sky, bright and blue.

[ac1.using_up.x08]
You hung it on the line to dry. Air moved, soft and slow, across its surface. It felt alive, still holding the weight of the water. The sun came out later, but not yet. The sky was bright and blue, like a promise. A breeze slipped through, lifting the edge.

[ac1.using_up.x09]
The sun came out. It warmed the air above the cloth. The water began to lift, soft and slow. The cloth felt lighter, alive with change. The breeze moved again, across the line. The edge of the cloth lifted, like a wing. The sky stayed bright, blue and true. The light touched the ground, warm and wide.

[ac1.using_up.x10]
The cloth dried in the warm air. The breeze moved again, across the line. The edge of the cloth lifted, like a wing. The sky stayed bright, blue and true. The light touched the ground, warm and wide. The cloth felt lighter, alive with change. The air above the cloth was clear and still.

[ac1.using_up.x11]
You tried to pour more, but nothing came out. The cup stayed empty, light and thin. The sky above grew darker, no longer bright. The breeze stopped, air still and thick. The cloth hung low, no longer alive. The ground felt cold, not warm or wide. The bucket stood empty, no liquid inside.

[ac1.using_up.x12]
The water was gone. No drop remained in the cup. The sky above stayed dark. The ground stayed cold, not warm or wide. The bucket stood empty, no liquid inside. The world felt still. No sound came from the trees. No bird sang. No child played. No one came near.

[ac1.using_up.x13]
The sun baked the floor dry. No drop remained in the cup. The sky above stayed dark. The ground stayed cold, not warm or wide. The bucket stood empty, no liquid inside. The world felt still. No sound came from the trees. No bird sang. No child played. No one came near.

[ac1.using_up.x14]
You looked for another bottle, but found none. The sky above stayed dark, no light to show. The floor stayed cold, not warm or wide. No child played, no bird sang, no one came near. Your hand reached out, fingers brushing the shelf. The empty bottle sat alone, forgotten. You thought of water, of rivers, of rain.

[ac1.using_up.x15]
The empty bottle sat on the shelf, forgotten. Water was gone, no drop left to flow. The air felt thick, like cloth pressed above. The shelf held nothing but dust and time. The sky stayed dark, no light to show. No child played, no bird sang, no one came near.
