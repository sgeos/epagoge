[m1.group.d00]
Put the ones that are alike together. Now you have a group.

[m1.group.d01]
A group is a set of things that belong together because they are alike.

[m1.group.s01]
The child found a pile of blue blocks and a set of red blocks.

[m1.group.s02]
The child put the blue blocks together in a group because they were alike.

[m1.group.s03]
The child then made a group of red blocks, also because they were alike.

[m1.group.s04]
The child placed the blue group on the floor and the red group on the table.

[m1.group.s05]
The child decided to make the groups equal in size.

[m1.group.s06]
The child moved one blue block from the blue group to the red group.

[m1.group.s07]
Now both groups had the same number of blocks.

[m1.group.s08]
The child felt glad and put the groups side by side.

[m1.group.s09]
The child knew the blocks were a set because they were chosen to be together.

[m1.group.x11]
The child looked at the two groups and smiled.

[m1.group.x12]
The blue and red blocks now matched in size and stayed side by side.

[m1.group.x13]
The child placed a small toy bear on top of the blue group and a toy boat on the red group.

[m1.group.x14]
The blocks stayed together, showing that alike things belong in the same set.

[m1.group.x15]
The child smiled and pushed the two equal groups side by side on the floor.
