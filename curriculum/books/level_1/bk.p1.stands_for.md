[p1.stands_for.d00]
The toy car is not a car. It stands for one.

[p1.stands_for.d01]
A toy that looks like a person, often used to play pretend.

[p1.stands_for.d02]
To no longer have something you had before.

[p1.stands_for.d03]
A small copy of something real, made to show how it looks.

[p1.stands_for.d27]
A piece of wood or plastic used for making things or playing games.

[p1.stands_for.d28]
To lose something, or not to win.

[p1.stands_for.d29]
A picture showing a place or thing.
