[c1.asking.x_danger.s02]
You ask about the hot stove. Something moves behind the stove. You feel a pull, like the stove is calling. You cannot look away. You think it might speak. It does not. You wonder if it knows you are afraid. The wall cracks again, wider now. You feel the danger close, alive.

[c1.asking.x_danger.s03]
