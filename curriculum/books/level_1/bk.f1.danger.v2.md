[f1.danger.v2.d00]
Some things hurt you. You cannot always see which. You have to be told.

[f1.danger.v2.d01]
A cut is a break in the skin caused by something sharp like a knife or glass.

[f1.danger.v2.d02]
Ill means not well; you feel sick and weak.

[f1.danger.v2.s01]
Carlo found a hot pan on the stove and reached for it.

[f1.danger.v2.s02]
He touched the hot pan and felt a sharp burn.

[f1.danger.v2.s03]
The burn made him cry and he felt sick.

[f1.danger.v2.s04]
He learned that danger can be hidden, like heat from a pan.

[f1.danger.v2.s05]
He now knows that not every hot thing is safe to touch.

[f1.danger.v2.s06]
He keeps his hands away from hot stoves and remembers to be careful.

[f1.danger.v2.s07]
He is not afraid to learn, even when he makes mistakes.

[f1.danger.v2.s08]
He knows that being careful helps you stay well and not get sick.

[f1.danger.v2.x12]
