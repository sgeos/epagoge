A toy car is not a car. It stands for one.

[p1.stands_for.x_written_record.s01]
The boy wrote a note in his diary about the toy car, saying it was a model of a real car. The boy kept the diary on a shelf above his bed. The car could roll but not fly, unlike birds in the sky. He drew a line to show the path the car took.

[p1.stands_for.x_written_record.s02]
He kept a list of all the things the toy car could do, like roll and turn, but it could not go far. It could not fly across the sky like a bird or swim in the river. It was made of metal and plastic, not wood or bamboo. It could not roll up the hill or down the path.

[p1.stands_for.x_written_record.s03]
One day, he lost the toy car, and he felt sad, so he wrote a note to remember it. He wrote the note on a small piece of paper. He placed it inside the diary to keep it safe. The diary was a book made of paper and cloth. It held his thoughts about the toy car.

[p1.stands_for.x_written_record.s04]
He found the car under the bed, and the diary said, “It was not lost. It was hidden.” He placed it back in the box, safe and still. The list said it was kept, even if it could not move. He closed the diary, and the cloth felt warm. He thought about the past, and how things change.

[p1.stands_for.x_written_record.s05]
He kept the toy car in a box, and the list said it was safe, even if it could not move. The box held the car, the diary, and the record of play. The list said it was kept, and that was enough. He thought about the past, and how things change.

[p1.stands_for.x_written_record.s06]
He smiled, and said, “This copy is not real, but it still holds my record of play.” He held the copy close, feeling its weight, though it was light. It could not move, but it still held the record of play. The list said it was safe, and he believed it.

The child made a model car from a block and named it "car" in a diary. It was built from blocks and painted blue with a red roof. The child believed it could fly, though it never left the ground. Each day, the diary recorded how it moved, slow and steady.

[p1.stands_for.x_written_record.s09]
Each day, the child wrote a note about the game, how the toy car moved, and what it could do. The child wrote about the toy car flying above the bed. It was able to move across the floor with a steady flow. The child believed it could carry a small apple and a blue balloon.

[p1.stands_for.x_written_record.s10]
The child kept a log of every time the car was lost, and each time, the child looked again, and found it under the bed. The child looked again under the bed, where the car had been before. The child thought about the toy car flying above the bed, but it was not there now.
