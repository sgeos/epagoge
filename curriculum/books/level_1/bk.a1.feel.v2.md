[a1.feel.v2.d01]
Afraid means feeling fear about something that might happen.

[a1.feel.v2.d02]
A feeling of being afraid about something bad happening.

He wants the toy. It sits on the shelf above the bed. He cannot reach it alone. He feels afraid to ask anyone. The toy is bright red and shaped like a bird. He has never seen it before. It looks alive, even when still. He tries to climb the chair but slips.

He walks across the room. His feet make soft sounds on the floor. He feels the air move around him. The toy is above the bed, still and bright. He cannot reach it alone. He thinks about asking anyone. But he is afraid to speak. He wants the toy more than anything.

[a1.feel.v2.x05]
The toy is on the shelf above the bed. He stands on the chair but cannot reach it. He tries to jump but lands hard on the floor. He looks around for help but sees no one. He wants the toy more than anything. He thinks about asking his sister but is afraid.

[a1.feel.v2.x06]
He reaches up but cannot touch it. He feels the air move as he tries again. He jumps, but his feet land on the floor. He looks around for help, but no one is near. He wants the toy more than anything else. He thinks about asking his sister, but is afraid.

[a1.feel.v2.x07]
He feels afraid because the toy is too high. He looks around for something to stand on, but nothing is near. He thinks about the chair again but fears falling. He hears a soft noise from the corner, like a whisper. He wonders if the toy is alive, watching him.

[a1.feel.v2.x08]
He tries to climb the chair but slips. He feels the floor rise up, then fall back. He lies on the ground, still afraid. The toy is still above, bright and far. He sees the corner, dark and quiet. He closes his eyes, tries to sleep. He hears a whisper, soft and low.

[a1.feel.v2.x09]

[a1.feel.v2.x10]
He picks it up and holds it close. The toy feels alive in his hands, warm and real. He breathes slow, like the breeze above the grass. No one else knows this gift, this secret, this promise. He wants the toy, not to play, but to keep. He believes it is his, not taken, not stolen.

[a1.feel.v2.x11]
He feels safe now, not afraid. He breathes slow, like the breeze above the grass. No one else knows this gift, this secret, this promise. He wants the toy, not to play, but to keep. He believes it is his, not taken, not stolen. He is able to stay still, not move, not run.
