[r1.where.v2.d00]
It is here, not there. It is over that way.

[r1.where.v2.d01]
Above means higher than something else in space, like a bird flying above the trees.

[r1.where.v2.d02]
Backward means moving in the opposite direction of forward, like stepping back.

[r1.where.v2.d03]
Behind means at the back of something, not in front, like a dog hiding behind a chair.

[r1.where.v2.d04]
Beside means next to something, not far away, like a book beside a bed.

[r1.where.v2.d05]
Direction means the way something is going, like forward or left.

[r1.where.v2.d06]
Following means coming after something, like a child following a parent.

[r1.where.v2.d07]
Moving toward the front, like a ball rolling forward.
