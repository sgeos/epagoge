[ac1.having.x_touching.d00]
The cup is mine. That one is hers. Now I have two.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s01]
I press my cup close, it belongs to me, and she rubs her cup, it is hers, and we both smile, knowing the gift is real.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s02]
We touch the cups again, and I feel my hand, my own, and she feels her hand, her own, and we share the same air, the same space, the same love.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s03]
I stroke the cup, soft and smooth, and she strokes hers, and we both feel the cool metal, the shine, the shape, and we think, this is not just a cup, it is a gift, a sign, a proof of care.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s04]
We place the cups on the table, side by side, and I say, “This one is mine,” and she says, “That one is hers,” and we both know, they belong to us, to us, to us.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s05]
She rubs my arm, soft, and I rub hers, and we both rub our cups, and the rubs are like a song, and we know this is not just a cup, it is a gift, and it belongs to us.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s06]
We tap our cups, one, two, and the tap is strong, and we hear the sound, and we smile, and I know my cup is mine, and hers is hers, and now we both feel rich, not for gold, but for this touch.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s07]
We press our cups together, and the press is strong, and the touch is real, and I feel safe, and she feels safe, and we know we are not alone, and we are not poor, but rich in this touch.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s08]
We stroke the cups, slow, and the stroke is soft, and we both stroke our own, and the stroke is like a breath, and we feel calm, and we know this is not just a thing, it is love, and it is ours.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s09]
We tap, press, rub, touch, stroke, tap again, and we know it is not about the cup, it is about the gift, and the touch, and the truth: mine, hers, ours, and the world feels right.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s10]
I press my cup close, it is mine, and she presses hers, it is hers, and we both know what it means to have something that belongs to us.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s11]
I rub my cup with care, it is mine, and she rubs hers, it is hers, and we both feel the smooth surface, the shape of our own.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s12]
I tap my cup gently, it is mine, and she taps hers, it is hers, and we both hear the sound that means it is ours, not shared.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s13]
I touch my cup again, it is mine, and she touches hers, it is hers, and we both know that even when we are together, each cup is own, not taken.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s14]
I hold my cup close, it is mine, and she holds hers, it is hers, and we both know that rich is not what you have, but what you feel, and poor is not what you lack, but what you think you need.

[ac1.having.x_touching.s15]
I press my cup into her hand, it is hers, and she presses mine into mine, it is mine, and we both know that love is not about who owns, but who cares.
