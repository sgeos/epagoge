[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.d00]
Living things need food and water to keep going. They grow.

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s01]
A baby bird asks for food, and its mother replies with a request to wait.

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s02]
The bird tries to grow, but feels weak and small.

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s03]
It cannot fly, so it shrinks in fear.

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s04]
The mother says, “I will help you grow.”

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s05]
She brings food and water, and the bird begins to feel better.

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s06]
It asks, “Can I fly now?” and the mother replies, “Yes, soon.”

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s07]
It lands, happy, and says, “I did it!” with a bright, clear voice.

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s08]
A baby bird asks for food, and its mother replies, "I will bring you what you need to grow."

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s09]
The bird feels weak and cannot fly. It asks again, "How can I grow strong?"

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s10]
The mother replies, "You must eat and drink to grow, not shrink."

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s11]
The bird tries to reach a high branch but falls. It says, "I need help!"

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s12]
The mother replies, "Hold on, I will lift you up."

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s13]
The bird grows bigger and stronger. It learns to fly and find food.

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s14]
It no longer asks, "How can I grow?" because it knows the answer.

[b1.keeping_going.x_asking.s15]
It sees a small bug stuck in mud. It says, "I will help you."
