[m1.compare.v2.d00]
These two are the same. That one is different. This pile has more.

[m1.compare.v2.d01]
Not the same, not alike, not equal.

[m1.compare.v2.d02]
Few means not many, a small number of things.

[m1.compare.v2.s01]
The two apples are alike, both red and round. They sit on the same table, side by side. They feel the same when touched. They were picked from the same tree. They share the same shape, same size. They belong to the same basket. They are made of the same material.

[m1.compare.v2.x04]
One apple is bigger than the other, but both are round. They share the same colour, red and bright. Both were picked from the same tree, same branch. They sit on the same table, side by side. They belong to the same basket, same place. They look alike, same shape, same size.

[m1.compare.v2.x05]
The red apple rolls faster than the green one. It moves across the floor, past the chair, and under the table. The green one stays still, not able to move. Both were picked from the same branch, same tree, same place. They look alike, same shape, same size, same colour.

[m1.compare.v2.x06]
A small ball fits inside the big basket. The ball is round, like the moon above. It rolls across the floor, just like the apple did. Both are alike in shape, same size, same place. The ball cannot move on its own, just like the apple. It stays still, waiting, no need to go.

[m1.compare.v2.x07]
The blue ball is heavier than the red one. It cannot roll across the floor like the apple did. Both are alike in shape, round and smooth. The blue ball stays still, not moving, just like the red one. They are equal in size, same weight in idea, not in fact.

[m1.compare.v2.x08]
A bird flies above the tree, but a cat stays below. The cat watches the bird, not afraid, just still. The bird has air, the cat has ground, both real. The bird flies, the cat waits, no need to go. Both are in place, not changing, not going ahead. The bird is above, the cat below, no need to compare.

[m1.compare.v2.x09]
The sun shines bright, but the clouds grow dark. The light feels warm, the air feels cold. The sky is blue above, the ground is brown below. Both are real, both are true. The wind blows, the leaves move. The bird flies, the cat watches. The tree stands still, the stream flows.

[m1.compare.v2.x10]
The water flows down the stream and into the river. The river knows what the stream cannot say. A fish swims ahead, and a leaf drifts behind. Both move, though one is still, and one is not. The same sky holds both above, the same ground below. The wind blows the same air, though it feels different.

[m1.compare.v2.x11]
The red apple rolls faster than the green one on the grass. Both roll along the same path, side by side. They move in the same direction, though one is bigger. The green apple is not afraid, though it feels slow. The red one does not try to win, yet it goes ahead.

[m1.compare.v2.x12]
They sit together on the table, side by side. They feel alike in shape, size, and weight. They share the same air, same light, same surface. They are both alive, both real, both whole. They belong in the same place, the same world. They are the same in every way.

[m1.compare.v2.x13]
The green apple looks smaller, but it feels heavier. The green apple feels heavier, though it looks smaller. Both share the same air, same light, same surface. They are alike in shape, size, and weight. No one can tell which one is better, just different. They are both whole, both real, both equal.

[m1.compare.v2.x14]
No one can tell which one is better, just different. They are alike in shape, size, and weight. Both are whole, both real, both equal. The red apple feels the same as the green one. They share the same air, same light, same surface. No change can make one better than the other.

[m1.compare.v2.x15]
The red apple rolls across the table and stops near the blue one. They are alike in shape, size, and weight. Both are whole, both real, both equal. The red apple feels the same as the green one. They share the same air, same light, same surface. No change can make one better than the other.
