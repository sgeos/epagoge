[f1.danger.q1.d00]
Some things hurt you. You cannot always see which. You have to be told.

[f1.danger.q1.d01]
A cut is a kind of harm that happens when something sharp touches your skin.

[f1.danger.q1.d02]
Harm is any kind of hurt that can happen to your body or mind.

[f1.danger.q1.d03]
Hot means something has heat that can burn your skin or cause harm.

[f1.danger.q1.d04]
Ill means you are sick and not able to feel well.

[f1.danger.q1.d05]
Safe means not in danger; it is a place or thing that does not cause harm.

[f1.danger.q1.a01]
Can you always see danger? No, you cannot always see danger, and you must be told when something is unsafe.

[f1.danger.q1.a02]
What happens when you are not careful? If you are not careful, you can get hurt or cause harm to yourself or others.

[f1.danger.q1.a03]
Why should you not touch something hot? You should not touch something hot because it can cause a burn, which is a kind of harm.

[f1.danger.q1.a04]
How do you know if someone is ill? You know someone is ill when they feel sick, cannot move well, or need help to feel better.

[f1.danger.q1.a05]
What should you do if you feel sick? If you feel sick, you should tell someone so they can help you and keep you safe.

[f1.danger.q1.a06]
Can you always tell when something will hurt you? No, you cannot always tell when something will hurt you.

[f1.danger.q1.a07]
What should you do when something feels hot? You should not touch it because hot things can cause harm.

[f1.danger.q1.a13]
What should you be when you touch a hot pan? You should be careful because hot things can harm your skin.

[f1.danger.q1.a14]
How do you know if you are not safe? You know you are not safe when you feel ill, sick, or hurt in a way that makes you need help.

[f1.danger.q1.a15]
What should you do when something feels hot? You should be careful because hot things can cause harm.
