The cup falls and you hear it.

[p1.sound.v3.d01]
A loud noise made when something hits a hard surface.

[p1.sound.v3.d02]
To hit your hands together quickly to make a noise.

[p1.sound.v3.d03]
Made with strong sound.

[p1.sound.v3.d04]
A sound that is not quiet or soft.

The cup fell from the table and made a loud bang. I heard the noise and called out to see what happened. It came from the kitchen where the table stood. I ran to the spot where the cup had broken. I picked it up with care, not wanting to cut my hand.
