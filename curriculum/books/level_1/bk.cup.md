[bk.cup.d01]
Things do what they do. You can make a thing do other things. This book is about that.

[bk.cup.d02]
Stuff is what a thing is made of.

[bk.cup.d03]
A cup is a thing you drink from.

[bk.cup.d04]
Water is a liquid you drink.

[bk.cup.d05]
A thing is empty when there is nothing in it.

[bk.cup.d06]
A thing is full when there is no room for more.

[bk.cup.d07]
To pour is to make a liquid go from one thing into another.

[bk.cup.s01]
The cup is empty. There is nothing in it.

[bk.cup.s02]
Pour the water in. Now the cup is full.

[bk.cup.s03]
Drink some of it. Now the cup is half full and half empty.

[bk.cup.s04]
Drink it all. Now the cup is empty again.

[bk.cup.s05]
Put soap in the water. Wash the cup with it.

[bk.cup.s06]
The soap water goes out into the sink. The cup is empty again.
