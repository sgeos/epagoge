[bk.cup.d01]
Things do what they do. You can make a thing do other things. This book is about that.

[bk.cup.d02]
Stuff is what a thing is made of.

[bk.cup.d03]
A cup is a thing you drink from.

[bk.cup.d04]
Water is a liquid you drink.

[bk.cup.d05]
A thing is empty when there is nothing in it.

[bk.cup.d06]
A thing is full when there is no room for more.

[bk.cup.d07]
To pour is to make a liquid go from one thing into another.

[bk.cup.s01]
The cup is empty. There is nothing in it. The cup sits on the table, still and quiet. No water, no liquid, no sign of life. The cup is made of clay, shaped by hand. It has a smooth rim, curved just right. It does not belong to anyone, not yet.

[bk.cup.s02]
Pour the water in. Now the cup is full. It fills the cup from bottom to rim. The cup holds the liquid, still and true. The clay warms from the inside. The cup feels alive, not just shaped. It sits, full and ready, on the table. No one has touched it yet.

[bk.cup.s03]
Drink some of it. Now the cup is half full and half empty. No one else is near, not even the dog. The cup does not speak, but it knows. It remembers the shape of the hand that held it. It knows the weight of what it once carried. It feels the heat of the sun through the glass.

[bk.cup.s04]
Drink it all. Now the cup is empty again. The cup lies still, no longer warm. It feels the air above its rim. No one comes to fill it again. It knows the weight of the hand that held it. It sees the sky through the glass. The sky is bright, but not kind.

[bk.cup.s05]
Put soap in the water. Wash the cup with it. The cup feels the slip of the hand that holds it. It sees the bubbles rise and break above the surface. The water flows around its rim, cool and clear. It remembers the shape of the hand that lifted it.

[bk.cup.s06]
The soap water goes out into the sink. The cup is empty again. The cup sits empty above the sink, still cool from the water. It feels the air above its rim, light and thin. No bubbles rise now, no slip of hand. The light from the window falls across its side.

[cup.x13]
The cup sits on the table, clean and dry. It feels the air above its rim, light and thin. No bubbles rise now, no slip of hand. The light from the window falls across its side. The cup is ready to hold something again. It does not know what will come, but it is able.

[cup.x14]
The cup is empty, but it is ready to hold something again. It feels the air above its rim, light and thin. No bubbles rise now, no slip of hand. The light from the window falls across its side. The cup is ready to hold something again. It does not know what will come, but it is able.
