[r1.unit.v4.d00]
This book is about three cups of water.

[r1.unit.v4.d01]
A cup is a small container used to hold liquid like water.

[r1.unit.v4.d02]
A unit is a single thing that can be counted or measured.

[r1.unit.v4.s01]
Three cups of water sat on the table.

[r1.unit.v4.x04]
A breeze blew through the window and tipped the nearest cup.

[r1.unit.v4.x05]
Water spilled across the table and dripped onto the floor.

[r1.unit.v4.x06]
The boy bent down to pick up the cup, his hand shaking.

[r1.unit.v4.x07]
He looked at the empty space where the water had been.

[r1.unit.v4.x08]
He sat on the floor and drew a circle with a stick.

[r1.unit.v4.x09]
Inside the circle, he wrote “three” in the dirt.

[r1.unit.v4.x10]
He whispered, “I will find more water,” and stood up.

[r1.unit.v4.x11]
He walked to the kitchen, ready to fill the cups again.

[r1.unit.v4.x12]
The other two cups stayed still, filled with clear water.

[r1.unit.v4.x13]
One cup tipped over and spilled water across the table.

[r1.unit.v4.x14]
The boy filled the cup from the tap, water rushing in with a steady flow.

[r1.unit.v4.x15]
He placed the cup back on the table, now full and steady, its surface bright and clear.
