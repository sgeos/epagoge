[p1.stands_for.v2.d00]
The toy car is not a car. It stands for one.

[p1.stands_for.v2.d01]
A large animal with fur that walks on four legs and lives in the forest.

[p1.stands_for.v2.d02]
A thing made to look exactly like another thing.

[p1.stands_for.v2.d03]
A toy that looks like a person and is used for playing.

[p1.stands_for.v2.d04]
To no longer have something you once had.

[p1.stands_for.v2.d05]
A small copy of something real, made to show how it looks.
