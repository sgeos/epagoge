[h1.resting.d00]
You did not check the first thing. So you have not checked the next one either.

[h1.resting.d01]
To stop moving or working; to be still.

[h1.resting.d104]
To be able to do something because someone or something helps.

[h1.resting.d105]
To stop moving or working, or to be calm.

[h1.resting.s01]
The boy forgot to check the door. The door was shut, but he thought it open. He felt afraid, though no one was near. His breath came short, his hands shook. He wanted to go back, but could not. He looked around, but saw no one. He had left the key inside.

[h1.resting.x05]
The wind blew the door shut behind him. He wanted to run, but his legs were weak. No one was near, yet he felt watched. He turned, looking back, but saw only the empty path. The door was locked, the key inside. He could not go back, could not go forward.

[h1.resting.x06]
He saw a red balloon floating above the trees. It drifted slowly, as if alive. The balloon moved ahead, just out of reach. He felt a pull, not strong, but real. He had never seen one so high, so bright. He thought of his sister, who loved red. But she was far away, and this was not her.

[h1.resting.x07]
He ran after it, thinking it was a gift. The balloon floated above the trees, bright red against the sky. He felt a pull, not strong, but real. He had never seen one so high, so bright. He thought of his sister, who loved red. But she was far away, and this was not her.

[h1.resting.x08]
He climbed the hill to see where it went. The air grew thin and cool above the trees. He had never seen a balloon so high, so bright. He thought of his sister, who loved red. But she was far away, and this was not her. He kept climbing, step by step, breath by breath.

[h1.resting.x09]
At the top, he found a small wooden box. It was old and smooth, with a lock that looked too small to be real. He tried to open it, but the lid would not move. He felt a cold wind blow across his face, though the air was still. He thought of his sister, who loved red, but she was far away.

[h1.resting.x10]
He opened it and found a key inside. He held the key, warm in his hand, though the air was cold. He thought of the box, the lock, the wind that had passed. He could not explain why, but he felt he must go ahead. He stepped forward, the ground soft under his feet.

[h1.resting.x11]
He turned around and saw his dog, asleep under a tree. He could not explain why, but he knew he must go ahead. He stood, his feet pressing into the soft earth. He looked back again, but the dog did not wake. He took one step, then another, away from the tree.

[h1.resting.x12]
The boy stood still, his breath slow and quiet. Something was wrong, though he could not say why. He had not checked the first thing. He remembered the dog, asleep under the tree. He had not checked if the dog was still alive. He had not asked if the dog was safe.

[h1.resting.x13]
He reached for the door, but it was locked and would not open. He stood still, his breath slow and quiet. The door would not open, though he tried again. He had not checked the first thing, and now he could not move. The air felt thick, like a wall pressing in.

[h1.resting.x14]
He looked around, afraid the door had been left open by mistake. He stepped forward, but the floor felt soft, like it might swallow him. His breath came fast, though he tried to calm himself. The room seemed smaller than before, the walls closing in. He reached back, fingers brushing the cloth, but the key was not there.

[h1.resting.x15]
The boy closed his eyes, then opened them again, trying to remember what he had forgotten. He remembered the key was not in his pocket. The door had been left open, and he had not checked. He tried to move, but his feet were stuck. The air felt thick, like it had weight.
