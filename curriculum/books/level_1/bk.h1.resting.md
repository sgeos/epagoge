[h1.resting.d00]
You did not check the first thing. So you have not checked the next one either.

[h1.resting.d01]
To stop moving or working; to be still.

[h1.resting.d104]
To be able to do something because someone or something helps.

[h1.resting.d105]
To stop moving or working, or to be calm.

[h1.resting.s01]
The boy forgot to check the door.

[h1.resting.x05]
The wind blew the door shut behind him.

[h1.resting.x06]

[h1.resting.x07]
He ran after it, thinking it was a gift.

[h1.resting.x08]

[h1.resting.x09]

[h1.resting.x10]

[h1.resting.x11]

[h1.resting.x12]
The boy stood still, his breath slow and quiet.

[h1.resting.x13]
He reached for the door, but it was locked and would not open.

[h1.resting.x14]
He looked around, afraid the door had been left open by mistake.

[h1.resting.x15]
The boy closed his eyes, then opened them again, trying to remember what he had forgotten.
