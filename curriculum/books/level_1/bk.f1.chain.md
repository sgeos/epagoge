A knock is a sound made when something hits a surface hard and fast.

The baby cried, and the mother ran to the door. She felt a cold breeze blow through the house. She saw nothing but the empty path and the quiet trees. She looked outside again, but no one was there. The sky was dark, and the stars seemed far away. She turned back inside, her feet dragging on the floor.

[f1.chain.s03]
She opened the door and saw a man with a basket. The basket held an apple, red and bright. She felt a pull, a need to take it. He said, “I brought an apple,” and handed it over. She took it, her fingers brushing the smooth skin. The apple felt warm, alive, like it had been waiting.
