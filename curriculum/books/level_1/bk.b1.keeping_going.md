[b1.keeping_going.d00]
Living things need food and water to keep going. They grow.

[b1.keeping_going.d01]
To take air in and push it out through the mouth or nose to stay alive.

[b1.keeping_going.d02]
To give food to someone or something to stay strong.

[b1.keeping_going.d03]
To become bigger or stronger over time.

[b1.keeping_going.d97]
To get smaller in size.

[b1.keeping_going.d103]
A thing that helps living things grow and stay alive.

[b1.keeping_going.d104]
To grow smaller in size.

[b1.keeping_going.s01]
The mother bird brought food every morning to feed her baby. The baby bird opened its eyes to see the bright sky above. It felt weak and needed food to stay alive. The baby bird tried to eat but could not move its head. It felt tired and wanted to sleep.

[b1.keeping_going.s02]
The baby bird grew bigger each day as it ate the food. It felt strong and alive, able to lift its head. The sky above was bright and warm. It blinked and looked around with new eyes. It felt the breeze touch its feathers. It could hear the wind in the trees.

[b1.keeping_going.s03]
One day it tried to fly and fell, but it did not hurt. It felt weak, but it knew it had to try again. The wind blew strong, pushing against its small body. It closed its eyes and thought about flying, not falling. It opened its eyes and saw the branch below, still safe.

[b1.keeping_going.s04]
The mother bird brought more food to help it grow stronger. The bird felt its body grow stronger after eating the food. It knew it had to keep trying to fly. The wind blew again, pushing against its small wings. It closed its eyes and thought about flying, not falling.

[b1.keeping_going.s05]
It learned to stay still when it heard a loud noise. The noise came from a car passing fast along the road. It stayed quiet, not moving, even when the wind blew. The air felt cold, and its body felt tired. It thought about the food it had eaten earlier.

[b1.keeping_going.s06]
It began to feel hungry again, so it called for food. The wind blew again, carrying leaves and dust. It closed its eyes, trying to stay calm. It thought about the food it had eaten earlier. It tried to stand, but its legs were weak. It looked up at the sky, bright and blue.

[b1.keeping_going.s07]
The mother bird flew back with a small supply of food. The baby bird opened its mouth wide. The mother bird stayed close, watching. The air was still, full of quiet. The baby bird could not move, but it tried. It thought about the sky, bright and blue. It felt weak, but not alone.

[b1.keeping_going.s08]
The baby bird ate the food and felt full and happy. The baby bird felt strong again after eating. The air above smelled of warm earth and green leaves. It wanted to fly, but first it had to rest. The mother bird stayed close, watching over it. The baby bird thought about food and water.

[b1.keeping_going.s09]
Then it flew away and found its own food. Then it flew above the green leaves and landed on a branch. The baby bird ate it, feeling full and alive. Water dripped from a leaf above, cool and clear. It drank from a small puddle below. It felt strong again, able to move ahead.
