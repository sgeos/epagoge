[h1.before.d00]
Nobody knew that once. Someone found it out.

[h1.before.d01]
New means not known before or not used yet.

[h1.before.d02]
Unknown means not known or not understood by anyone.

[h1.before.s01]
A child found a small box hidden under a tree.

[h1.before.s02]
The child opened it and found a red stone inside.

[h1.before.s03]
The child held it close, feeling safe and calm.

[h1.before.s04]
No one had ever seen such a thing before, so it was new.

[h1.before.s05]
The child told no one, afraid to be laughed at.

[h1.before.s06]
But the stone seemed to know, and it whispered a name.

[h1.before.s07]
The child knew that secrets could be safe, and love could grow.

[h1.before.x10]
At the edge of the clearing, the child saw a shadow move, and a small wooden boat floated on the still water.

[h1.before.x11]
The child whispered back, “I am not afraid,” and the world felt whole again.

[h1.before.x12]
The child ran through the forest, following a path only the stone seemed to know.

[h1.before.x13]
The wooden boat drifted closer, and the child stepped in, feeling the water carry them forward.

[h1.before.x14]

[h1.before.x15]
