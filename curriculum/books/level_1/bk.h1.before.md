[h1.before.d00]
Nobody knew that once. Someone found it out.

[h1.before.d01]
New means not known before or not used yet.

[h1.before.d02]
Unknown means not known or not understood by anyone.

[h1.before.s01]
A child found a small box hidden under a tree. The box was made of wood and had a lock that looked old. No one had ever seen this box before. It did not belong to any family in the village. The child could not explain why it was there.

[h1.before.s02]
The child opened it and found a red stone inside. It felt warm, even though the air was cold. No one had ever seen a stone like this before. It did not belong to any place on earth. The child held it close, feeling safe and calm. It was something older, something deeper, something secret.

[h1.before.s03]
The child held it close, feeling safe and calm. No one had ever seen such a thing before, so it was new. It felt alive, though it was made of stone. No one could explain it, not even the oldest books. The child felt it was meant to be found, not lost.

[h1.before.s04]
No one had ever seen such a thing before, so it was new. It felt alive, though it was made of stone. No one could explain it, not even the oldest books. The child felt it was meant to be found, not lost. It was warm, even in the cold air.

[h1.before.s05]
The child told no one, afraid to be laughed at. No one else could see what the stone seemed to know. It whispered a name, soft and clear, though no mouth moved. No one had ever made such a thing, not even the oldest books. The child knew it was meant to be found, not lost.

[h1.before.s06]
But the stone seemed to know, and it whispered a name. The name was not spoken, yet it lived in the air. It felt like a secret meant to be kept, not shared. The child could not explain how they knew it was real. The stone had seen things no one else could.

[h1.before.s07]
The child knew that secrets could be safe, and love could grow. Each step forward felt heavier, yet the path ahead stayed clear. The stone had not lied; it had only known what others could not see. The child did not look back, though the past called like a lost name.

[h1.before.x10]
At the edge of the clearing, the child saw a shadow move, and a small wooden boat floated on the still water. No one else was near, not even a bird or a bug in the tall grass. The boat did not drift, but moved toward the child, as if it knew a name.

[h1.before.x11]
The child whispered back, “I am not afraid,” and the world felt whole again. A bird flew above, its wings cutting the air like a knife. The shadow moved again, not from the trees, but from the water. A small apple floated near the boat, its skin bright red. No one else had ever seen this place, not even the old stone.

[h1.before.x12]
The child ran through the forest, following a path only the stone seemed to know. A bird cried above, its voice sharp and clear. The path bent ahead, marked by small stones placed in a line. No one had ever come here before, not even the old stone. The child felt alive, though tired, and kept moving forward.

[h1.before.x13]
The wooden boat drifted closer, and the child stepped in, feeling the water carry them forward. The boat drifted on the bright water, and the child felt the air move above. The sky turned blue, and the sun rose higher, warming the wooden sides. The child looked ahead, not knowing what came next, but feeling alive.

[h1.before.x14]
Before this day, nobody knew what was past the water. The air felt different, thicker, like something alive was breathing above. The sky turned dark, not with clouds, but with something else—shadows moving fast. A sound came from below, like a bell ringing deep in the earth. The child felt alone, but not afraid.

[h1.before.x15]
Now the child knows, because the child went and looked. The shadows moved like animals, not afraid, but watching. The bell was not from the earth, but from a stone deep in the ground. No one had been there before, not even the oldest tree. The sky changed, not dark, but bright with stars that had never been seen.
