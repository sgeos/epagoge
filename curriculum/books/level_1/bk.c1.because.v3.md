It fell because you let go. First this, so then that.

[c1.because.v3.d01]
Instead means a word that shows one thing happens in place of another.

[c1.because.v3.d02]
Way means a path or method by which something is done or happens.

The balloon floated up into the air because you let go. It rose above the garden, moving slowly, as if it had a mind of its own. It drifted across the sky, bright and light, like a bubble made of air. No one could catch it, not even the tallest child.

[c1.because.v3.s03]
You watched it drift across the sky, thinking you could catch it again. It fell because you let go. The balloon drifted down, slow and soft, like a feather. [c1.because.v3.s04]

You thought about the way it floated, bright and round, alive in the sky. It was not the balloon that was gone, but the moment you let it go.
