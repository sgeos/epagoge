[n1.keep_trying.q1.a01]

[n1.keep_trying.q1.a05]
Can you try again if you are not sure? Yes, you can try again even if you are not sure, because trying helps you find out.

[n1.keep_trying.q1.a06]
Can you quit when things feel too hard? You can quit, but quitting might mean you never finish.

[n1.keep_trying.q1.a07]

[n1.keep_trying.q1.a08]
Does trying again mean you are weak? No, trying again means you are strong, not weak.

[n1.keep_trying.q1.a09]
Can you try again if you feel bad? Yes, you can try again even if you feel bad, because trying helps you feel better.

[n1.keep_trying.q1.a10]
What happens if you stop before you finish? You stop before you finish, and you never see what you could have done.

[n1.keep_trying.q1.a11]
What should you do when you feel like giving up? You should try again, even when you feel like giving up.

[n1.keep_trying.q1.a12]
What does it mean to keep trying while you still can? It means to not stop until you have done all you can.

[n1.keep_trying.q1.a13]

[n1.keep_trying.q1.a14]
What do you do when it looks impossible? You try again, because trying again can make the impossible possible.

[n1.keep_trying.q1.a15]
Can you quit when it feels too hard? You should not quit, because you might still be able to try again.
