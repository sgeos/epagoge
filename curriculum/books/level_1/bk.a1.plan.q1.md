[a1.plan.q1.d00]
Before you do it, you think how. Then you do it that way.

[a1.plan.q1.a01]
How do you decide what to do? You make a plan before you begin.

[a1.plan.q1.a02]
What is a plan for? A plan helps you do something step by step.

[a1.plan.q1.a03]
What happens if you do not plan? You may do it wrong or not finish.

[a1.plan.q1.a04]
Can you change your plan? Yes, you can change your plan if needed.

[a1.plan.q1.a05]
How do you follow your plan? You do each step in order, one by one.

[a1.plan.q1.a06]
Can you do something without a plan? Yes, but it may not work well.

[a1.plan.q1.a07]
What do you do when you finish your plan? You check if it worked and stay ready.

[a1.plan.q1.a08]
How do you learn from your plan? You think about what worked and what to fix.

[a1.plan.q1.a09]
What do you do before you start? You think about the method you will use.

[a1.plan.q1.a10]
How do you know what to do? You follow your plan to do it that way.

[a1.plan.q1.a11]
How do you start a new task? You make a plan and follow the method.

[a1.plan.q1.a12]
Can you change your plan? Yes, you can change your plan if you need to.

[a1.plan.q1.a13]
What do you do before you start? You make a plan to think about how to do it.

[a1.plan.q1.a14]
How do you use your plan? You follow your plan so you do it the right way.

[a1.plan.q1.a15]
What happens if you do it that way? You do it with care, and it works because you planned ahead.
