The water is in the cup. Now it is out of the cup.

[p1.inside.v4.d01]
A thing that holds something inside, like a cup or a box.

[p1.inside.v4.d02]
To go into something from outside.

[p1.inside.v4.d03]
To be the right size for something, so it stays in place.

[p1.inside.v4.s01]
The water is in the cup. It fits just right, not too much, not too little. The cup holds the water, and the water stays inside. The cup is not empty, not yet, not ever. It waits, still, full, for what comes next. The water feels cool, smooth, alive. The cup is not made of glass, not metal, not wood.

[p1.inside.v4.x05]
The cup is empty. No water remains inside. It sits alone on the table. No one touches it. No one sees it. The air above it is still. The light shines down, bright and clear. The cup does not move. It does not think. It knows nothing. It has no name.

[p1.inside.v4.x06]
A child picks up the cup. The cup feels warm from the sun above. She moves it slowly, careful not to spill. She walks across the room, step by step. The air is still, but not quiet. A bird outside sings, high and clear. The cup is full now, heavy with meaning.

[p1.inside.v4.x07]
She puts it on the table. The table is smooth and warm from the sun above. A small bubble rises, slow and clear. The water is not empty, not lost, not gone. It stays, held by the cup, held by the table, held by the air. No one moves it, no hand touches it again.

[p1.inside.v4.x09]
