[r1.written.d00]
Make a mark so you know later. The mark stays after you forget.

[r1.written.d01]
A copy is a thing made to look like another thing, like a note written again.

[r1.written.d41]
A log is a record of times or things done, kept for later use.

[r1.written.d44]
A tally is a mark used to count, often in sets of five.

[r1.written.s01]
I made a mark on the paper so I would know later. I drew it small, just above the list. It showed I had thought about it before. I did not forget, because the mark stayed. The mark was not loud, but it was clear. It meant something, even if no one else saw.

[r1.written.s02]
I wrote a note about the day in my diary. The words felt real, like they belonged to me. I wrote about the boy who laughed too loud. I wrote about the bird that flew across the yard. I wrote about the way the wind moved the leaves. I wrote about the sound the door made when it closed.

[r1.written.s03]
I kept a list of things I needed to do. Each task felt real, like it belonged to me. I wrote about the apple I had to pick from the tree. I noted the door I must close after leaving. I marked the step I must take to fix the broken chair.

[r1.written.s04]
I recorded each step in my log. Each mark showed where I had been. The pen wrote a line across the page. I made a small circle to show the apple I picked. I drew a line below the door I closed. The log held my story, clear and real.

[r1.written.s05]
I made a tally to count the days. Each mark stood alone, clear and real. The pen moved slowly, one by one. I counted each day with care. The marks grew longer, line by line. They showed how time had passed. I made each line straight and true. The numbers stayed correct and clear.

[r1.written.s06]
I copied the note so I would not lose it. The paper felt smooth, like a leaf from a tree. I held the pen steady, each letter clear and true. I wrote the words exactly as they were meant to be. No word was changed, no letter was left out.

[r1.written.s07]
I kept the record safe in the box. The box sat on the shelf, above the floor, where light could reach it. I made a mark on the paper inside, a small line, so I would know later. The mark was not big, but it was clear, like a path through the dark.

[r1.written.s08]
The mark stayed even when I forgot the day. The paper held the line like a promise. I had made it to remember, not to lose. It stayed above the noise, above the time. It was not about the day, but about being sure. I could not feel it, but I knew it was there.

[r1.written.s09]
The list, the log, and the note all stayed true. I could not see it, but I knew it was there. The paper held the line like a promise. It stayed above the noise, above the time. It was not about the day, but about being sure. I could not feel it, but I knew it was there.

[r1.written.s42]
He used a tally to count how many times his dog made noise. He made a mark so he knew later. The tally stayed true above the noise. He counted in the quiet, alone. The paper held the line like a promise. It was not about the dog, but about being sure.

[r1.written.s43]
He counted how many times he laughed that day. Each laugh made a mark above the quiet. The paper held the line like a promise. He made the mark so he knew later. The mark stayed true above the noise. It was not about the laugh, but about being sure.

[r1.written.x15]
He found the box under the bed and opened it to see the marks, the list, and the note all still there. Each mark told a story he could not forget. The list showed what he had tried to do. He felt the paper, soft and thin, like skin. The marks were not about the past, but about being alive.
