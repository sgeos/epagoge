[r1.written.d00]
Make a mark so you know later. The mark stays after you forget.

[r1.written.d01]
A copy is a thing made to look like another thing, like a note written again.

[r1.written.d41]
A log is a record of times or things done, kept for later use.

[r1.written.d44]
A tally is a mark used to count, often in sets of five.

[r1.written.s01]
I made a mark on the paper so I would know later.

[r1.written.s02]
I wrote a note about the day in my diary.

[r1.written.s03]
I kept a list of things I needed to do.

[r1.written.s04]
I recorded each step in my log.

[r1.written.s05]
I made a tally to count the days.

[r1.written.s06]
I copied the note so I would not lose it.

[r1.written.s07]
I kept the record safe in the box.

[r1.written.s08]
The mark stayed even when I forgot the day.

[r1.written.s09]
The list, the log, and the note all stayed true.

[r1.written.s42]
He used a tally to count how many times his dog made noise.

[r1.written.s43]
He counted how many times he laughed that day.

[r1.written.x15]
He found the box under the bed and opened it to see the marks, the list, and the note all still there.
