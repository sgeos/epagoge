[f1.parts.d00]
A thing is made of parts. One bad part can stop the whole thing.

[f1.parts.d01]
A part is a piece that helps make a whole thing.

[f1.parts.d02]
A whole is all of something, not missing any part.

[f1.parts.s01]
A boy built a car from blocks and a wheel. The car had a body made of wood and a roof shaped like a plate. The wheel rolled above the ground and turned fast. The wheel was round and smooth, like a circle. He built it to roll on the path, not to stop.

[f1.parts.s02]
The car was bright red and could roll fast on the path. Each part worked together to make it move. The wheel rolled above the ground and turned fast. The body held everything in place, strong and broad. The roof shaped like a plate kept the air out. The blocks were joined with glue and care.

[f1.parts.s03]
He pushed it, and it moved, but then a block broke. He pushed it again, but the broken block stayed apart. The car could not roll ahead because one part was missing. He thought about the past, how the blocks were joined with care. He tried to fix it, but his hands were too small, too weak.

[f1.parts.s04]
The bad part made the whole car stop. The broken block could not join again. He tried to fit it back, but the shape was wrong. The car would not roll forward, not even a little. He looked at the pieces, all apart and alone. No glue could fix what was broken.

[f1.parts.s05]
The boy felt sad and could not fix it with glue. He looked at the parts, all apart and alone. Each piece had a shape that did not fit. He tried to join them again, but the parts were wrong. No glue could make them alike. The car would not roll, not even a little.

[f1.parts.s06]
He tried again with a new block, but it did not fit. The block was too short to reach the next part. He felt stuck, unable to move ahead. He looked at the block and saw its shape was wrong. It did not match the space above it. He tried to push it in, but it slipped away.

[f1.parts.s07]
He learned that one bad part can stop the whole thing. He knew the block was broken and could not be fixed. The thing would not work without a proper part. He looked at the space above and saw it needed something strong. He felt afraid to try again, but knew he had to.

[f1.parts.x10]
The boy looked at the broken block and thought about what to do next. He knew the block was part of a larger thing that needed to work. Each part had a job, and no part could be missing. He saw a piece of wood that looked alike in shape and size.

[f1.parts.x11]
He asked his sister if she had a block that fit just right. She looked around the room and found a block made of wood. It was alike in shape and size, and looked just right. He took it from her hand and held it close. He felt the block was part of something bigger, something alive.

[f1.parts.x12]
The boy looked at the broken block and thought hard about what to do next. He knew the block was part of something bigger, something alive. He could not fix it alone, but he could try again. He thought about the toy box, the blocks inside, the ones that fit just right.

[f1.parts.x13]
He went to the toy box and found a block that was just the right size and shape. He picked up the block and felt its shape. The block was a piece of something alive, not broken. He placed it above the others, making a new shape. He stepped back and saw how they worked together.

[f1.parts.x14]
The boy looked at the broken block and thought about how to fix it. He knew he had to find a way to make it whole again. He found a small block, same shape, same size, same colour. He placed it above the crack, careful not to press too hard.

[f1.parts.x15]
The car rolled again, smooth and fast, down the path. No part was missing, no piece was broken. The path was clear, the air was still, and the sun shone bright. The boy watched from the side, not afraid, just glad. He knew the car was alive, not just metal, but a thing made of parts.
