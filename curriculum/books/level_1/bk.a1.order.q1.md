[a1.order.q1.d00]
Open the box first. Then take out the cup. Not the other way.

[a1.order.q1.a01]
What should you do first? Open the box first.

[a1.order.q1.a02]
What comes after opening the box? Then take out the cup.

[a1.order.q1.a03]
Why not take out the cup first? Because you must follow the correct order.

[a1.order.q1.a04]
What happens if you take out the cup too soon? You break the step order.

[a1.order.q1.a05]
Can you skip the first step? No, you must follow the order.

[a1.order.q1.a06]
What does the order tell you to do? It tells you the right step to do.

[a1.order.q1.a07]
What is the next step after opening the box? The next step is to take out the cup.

[a1.order.q1.a08]
Is the cup ready before the box is open? No, the cup is not ready until the step is done.

[a1.order.q1.a09]
What do you do after taking out the cup? You can use it, but only after the step.

[a1.order.q1.a10]
Can you change the order? No, the order must stay the same.

[a1.order.q1.a11]
What happens if you take out the cup before opening the box? You cannot do it right, because the step is wrong.

[a1.order.q1.a12]
What must you do to follow the right order? You must do each step in the right order.

[a1.order.q1.a13]
Can you take out the cup before the box is open? No, you cannot, because that breaks the order.

[a1.order.q1.a14]
What should you do first when opening the box? Open the box first, because that is the correct order.

[a1.order.q1.a15]
What happens if you take out the cup before opening the box? You cannot take out the cup before opening the box, because the box must be open first, and that is the right step.
