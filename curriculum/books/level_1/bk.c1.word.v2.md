[c1.word.v2.d00]
A word is a thing you say. A name is a word for one thing.

[c1.word.v2.d01]
A letter is a symbol that helps spell a word.

[c1.word.v2.d02]
To spell means to say the letters of a word in order.

[c1.word.v2.d03]
A term is a word used to name a thing or idea.

[c1.word.v2.d04]
A word is a thing you say to mean something.
