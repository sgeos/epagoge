[b1.animals.q1.d04]

What is a dog? A dog is an animal that is alive and moves.

What part of the body helps a dog hear? A dog hears with its ears, which are on the side of its head.

[b1.animals.q1.a05]
What kind of animal is a dog? A dog is an animal that is alive, moves, and has eyes to see.

[b1.animals.q1.a06]
How does a dog move? A dog moves using its body and legs, just like other animals.

[b1.animals.q1.a07]
Why is a dog able to live? A dog lives because it has a body, eyes to see, and ears to hear.
