The key is metal. Metal is one of the things a thing can be made of.

[p1.metal.d01]
Copper is a metal that is red and can be used to make things like wires.

[p1.metal.d02]
Iron is a metal that is strong and can be used to build things like nails.

[p1.metal.d03]
Steel is a strong metal made from iron and other things.

[p1.metal.s01]
The key is metal and it is made of steel. It feels cold and hard against the palm. The steel key is strong and will not bend. The lock is metal and made of steel too. The light inside is bright and warm. The room is quiet and full of old things.

[p1.metal.s02]
The steel key was cold and hard, and it fit into the lock. A warm breeze blew through the gap. A wooden table sat in the corner, covered in a thick blanket. A single apple rested on the table, shiny and red. A shadow moved slowly across the wall. The key felt heavier in my hand.

[p1.metal.s03]
The lock turned, and the door opened to a room with a wooden table. The metal key felt warm against my palm as I stepped inside. I saw a small blue bowl, half-full of water, and a red apple resting nearby. A metal ring hung from a nail, its surface bright and clean.

[p1.metal.s04]
The ring was warm and bright, and it looked like it had never rusted. It felt alive, not just metal, but something older, something real. I could not explain it, but I knew it had been waiting. It looked like it belonged to someone, or maybe to no one. The ring held a secret, not written, not spoken, but felt.

[p1.metal.s05]
He picked it up and the metal felt alive, like it knew him. It looked like it had been made for a hand like his, not any other. He turned it over, the edge catching the light, bright as a new moon. It felt older than the house, older than the earth.

[p1.metal.s06]
He placed it on the table and the room grew quiet, as if the air had stopped. No sound came from outside, not even the wind. It had been made to fit a lock, not just any door. It was not broken, not bent, not worn. It was alive, and it was waiting.

[p1.metal.s07]
The key was still in the lock, and the door stayed open. The metal felt warm, though the air was cold. It was not just a tool, but alive in a way that could not be named. The room held its breath, as if waiting for something to begin. No sound came from outside, not even the wind.

[p1.metal.x11]
He reached for the key, still in the lock, and turned it once more. He turned the key again, but the metal stayed stuck inside the lock. The room felt heavier, as if the air had grown thick with waiting. He thought of the apple in the basket, half-eaten, left behind.

[p1.metal.x12]
He turned to close the door, but the key would not come out. He tried to pull the key out, but it stayed stuck, metal cold against his fingers. The air above the lock felt thick, like breath held too long. He thought of the apple, now gone, and the basket left behind.

[p1.metal.x13]
The air felt thick, and the walls seemed to breathe with him. The key was metal, cold and hard, like a piece of the earth itself. The lock was not broken, not cracked, not even bent. It was just... He thought of the apple, gone, and the basket, left behind.

[p1.metal.x14]
The key felt heavier now, as if it had grown into the lock itself. He could not move it, not even a little. Something deep inside the wall knew his name. His breath came short, his hands shook. He thought of the apple, gone, and the basket, left behind. The lock was not broken, not cracked, not even bent.
