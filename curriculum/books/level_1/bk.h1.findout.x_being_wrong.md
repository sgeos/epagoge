How do you know? Let us go and find out. Now we have settled it.

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s01]
A child wants to know if a blue ball is true, so they ask, and someone says it is false, but they decide to find proof.

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s02]
The child sees a blue ball and says, “It is true.”

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s03]
Someone says, “No, it is wrong.”

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s04]
They tell others, “It is not false, it is true.”

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s05]
They know now because the proof was clear.

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s06]
They feel glad, and the story ends.

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s07]
False ideas can hurt, but proof helps us decide what is true.

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s08]
We asked for proof, and found the ball was not food, so we knew it was wrong.

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s09]
We decided to trust proof, not guesses, and that settled our doubt.

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s10]
We told others, and they agreed, so now we all know the truth.

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s11]
We learned to check, not just believe, and that helps us decide what is right.

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s12]
We keep asking, “Is this true?” and use proof to settle every doubt.

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s13]
Now we know: false ideas can hide, but truth stays, and proof proves it.

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s14]
We decide to find proof that the sky is blue, not false, not wrong.

[h1.findout.x_being_wrong.s15]
We look up and see the sky is bright blue, not red, not black, not wrong.
