[i1.manners.d00]
This book is about saying hello, please, thank you, I am sorry, and goodbye.

[i1.manners.d01]
Bye means saying goodbye to someone when you leave.

[i1.manners.d02]
Greet means to say hello to someone when you meet.

[i1.manners.d03]
Hello means saying “I see you” when you first meet someone.

[i1.manners.d05]
Please means asking someone to do something in a kind way.

[i1.manners.d06]
Sorry means saying you are not happy about something you did.

[i1.manners.d07]
Thank means saying you are happy for something someone did.

[i1.manners.s01]
Hello, I say to my friend when I see them.

[i1.manners.s02]
Hi, they reply, and we greet each other with a smile.

[i1.manners.s03]
I ask, please, can you help me carry this bag?

[i1.manners.s04]
They say yes, and help me carry it across the yard.

[i1.manners.s05]
I feel glad because I was kind and they were kind too.

[i1.manners.s06]
I say, sorry, I broke your glass, and I am afraid you will be angry.

[i1.manners.s18]
They say no harm was done and I feel better.

[i1.manners.s07]
We walk back to the house, and I say, goodbye, see you tomorrow.

[i1.manners.s08]
They wave and say, bye, and I wave back.
