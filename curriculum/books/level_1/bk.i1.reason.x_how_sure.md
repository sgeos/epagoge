[i1.reason.x_how_sure.d00]
This book is about doubt, maybe, sure, and why we accept or agree.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s01]
A child sees a red ball float above the ground and says, “That is right because it floats.” The ball moves in the air, not falling, not breaking. It floats with no rope, no strings, no help. He believes the ball can live in the air, like a bird. He wants to touch it, to hold it, to know why.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s02]
The child doubts the ball can fly. He cannot explain how it floats without air. He knows no ball is able to live in the sky like a bird. He looks around for help, but no one agrees. He feels alone, afraid to ask again. He thinks maybe it is magic, but he knows that is not true.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s03]
Maybe it is magic, but that is not true because no ball floats without air. The ball floats because air lifts it, not magic. No ball can live in the sky without air. I am sure this is real, not a dream. I cannot hide my doubt, but I must believe.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s04]
Why do you say so? The child says, “I saw it rise, so I must believe.” I agree because I saw it rise above the ground. I am sure it is real, not a dream. No ball floats without air, and I know that. I cannot hide my doubt, but I must believe.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s05]
Tell it as one story: the ball is not alive, but it moves because wind blows. The ball moves because the breeze lifts it, not because it is alive. I know it is not magic, but the air is strong enough to carry it. No one else can see the wind, but I believe it is real.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s06]
The child agrees the ball floats only because the breeze lifts it. The breeze is real, not magic, and I can feel it. I was afraid before, but now I know it is true. The wind moves things, just like air lifts the balloon. No one else sees it, but I believe it.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s07]
The child knows, “I was wrong before, but now I understand.” I am sure about the breeze because I feel it move the balloon. Air lifts things, just like the wind lifts the leaf. I believe what I see, even if no one else does. I can feel the air, and it is real.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s08]
I feel doubt about the apple because it is red and round like a ball. I am not sure about the apple because it looks like a ball. I feel afraid to touch it, even though it is red and round. I know I should not be afraid, but I cannot help it.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s09]
Maybe the apple is safe because it grows on a tree. I am able to feel calm when I think about it. I can see it growing above the ground. It is red and shiny, like a small sun. I do not need to be afraid of it. I can touch it, and it will not hurt.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s10]
I am sure it is not true because the apple is not a ball. It is not a ball because it is not round like a ball. It cannot roll like a ball. It grows on a tree, not in a game. It is not used to play, but to eat.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s11]
Why do you say so? I cannot agree with that. I am not sure I can agree about the apple being only food. It can be used to play, like a ball, if you roll it. I have seen a child roll an apple across the yard. I do not believe it is only for eating.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s12]
I accept that the apple is food, not a ball. It is right to agree when the truth is clear. I am able to say what I believe, even if it is hard. I stand by my words, even if others disagree. I am not afraid to be alone in my truth.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s13]
I am not afraid to tell the truth because it is right. I am sure the truth is right. I believe what I know, even when others doubt. I know what is fair and what is wrong. I will not lie, even if it helps me. I trust my own thoughts, clear and true.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s14]
I do not want to lie or cheat, so I say no to wrong. I am sure I can do what is right. I will not agree to something wrong, even if it is hard. I know the truth is strong and clear. I will not lie, even if others ask me to.

[i1.reason.x_how_sure.s15]
I will not hide the fact because I want to be honest. I am sure I can do what is right. I will not agree to something wrong, even if it is hard. I know the truth is strong and clear. I will not lie, even if others ask me to.
