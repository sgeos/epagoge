[f1.alive.v3.d00]
Living things die. Dying does not go backwards.

[f1.alive.v3.d01]
Alive means being able to eat, drink, move, and feel, not dead.

[f1.alive.v3.d02]
Asleep means lying down, eyes closed, not awake, and not moving.

[f1.alive.v3.d03]
When a person or animal is awake, they can see, hear, and think.

[f1.alive.v3.d04]
Dead means not alive, not breathing, not moving, and never waking.

[f1.alive.v3.d05]
Death is the end of life, when a living thing stops being alive.

[f1.alive.v3.d06]
Die means to stop living, to no longer be alive, to never come back.

[f1.alive.v3.d07]
Drink means to take liquid into the mouth and swallow it.

[f1.alive.v3.s04]

[f1.alive.v3.s05]

[f1.alive.v3.s06]
