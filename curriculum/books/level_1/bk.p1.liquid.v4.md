[p1.liquid.v4.d00]
Water takes the shape of the cup. The empty cup is full of air.

[p1.liquid.v4.d01]
Blow means to push air out with your mouth, like when you make a bubble.

[p1.liquid.v4.d02]
Breath is the air you take in and let out when you breathe.

[p1.liquid.v4.d03]
Bubble is a round ball of air inside liquid, like when you blow into water.

[p1.liquid.v4.d04]
Drank means to take liquid into the mouth and swallow it.

[p1.liquid.v4.d05]
Flow is the way liquid moves, like water in a stream.

[p1.liquid.v4.s01]
The cup is empty and full of air.

[p1.liquid.v4.x07]
The cup sits on the table, still full of air.

[p1.liquid.v4.x08]
A breeze blows, and the air inside the cup moves.

[p1.liquid.v4.x09]
Now the cup is empty, ready to hold water.

[p1.liquid.v4.x10]
The water takes the shape of the cup, smooth and clear.

[p1.liquid.v4.x11]
The cup is full, and the water does not spill.

[p1.liquid.v4.x12]
The cup is now empty again, and the child puts it away.

[p1.liquid.v4.x13]
The cup is filled with water, and the water takes the shape of the cup.

[p1.liquid.v4.x14]
The water flows slowly down the drain when the cup is tipped.

[p1.liquid.v4.x15]
The empty cup sits on the table, waiting to be filled again.
