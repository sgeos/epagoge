Someone told you. Telling can be wrong. Go and look.

[f1.check.q1.a01]
Did someone tell you the truth? Someone might lie, so you must check the facts.

[f1.check.q1.a02]
Can you trust what someone says without looking? No, you must check to know the truth.

[f1.check.q1.a03]
Is it clever to believe everything you hear? No, it is clever to learn by looking yourself.

[f1.check.q1.a04]
How do you know if a story is true? You must learn by checking what is real.

[f1.check.q1.a05]
What if you see something different than what was told? Then go and look again—truth is what you see.

[f1.check.q1.a06]
Did someone tell you the sky is green? No, someone told you the sky is green, but you must check the sky yourself.

[f1.check.q1.a07]
Can you know the truth without checking? No, you cannot know the truth without checking, because lying is possible.

[f1.check.q1.a08]
Did someone tell you the apple is red? The apple might be red, but you must check to know for sure.

[f1.check.q1.a09]
Can you know the apple is red without looking? No, you cannot know the apple is red without looking, because telling can be wrong.

[f1.check.q1.a10]
What should you do if you are told the apple is red but it looks green? You should check it again, because telling can be wrong, and only looking can show the truth.

[f1.check.q1.a11]
Is it clever to believe what someone says without checking? No, it is not clever to believe without checking, because you might learn a lie.

[f1.check.q1.a12]
What happens if you try to learn without checking? You might learn a lie, and that can cause harm or mistake.

[f1.check.q1.a13]
Can you learn the truth without staring at the apple? No, you cannot learn the truth without staring at the apple, because only seeing it shows what is real.

[f1.check.q1.a14]
What should you do if you still doubt after looking? You should try again, check again, and keep learning until you know the truth for yourself.

[f1.check.q1.a15]
Why should you check before you believe? Because telling can be wrong, and checking helps you know the truth.
