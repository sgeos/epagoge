[c1.know.v3.d01]
To forget means to no longer remember something you once knew.

[c1.know.v3.d02]
To knew means to have known something in the past, even if you no longer remember it now.

[c1.know.v3.d03]
I thought means I had an idea in my mind earlier.

[c1.know.v3.d04]
To understand means to know the meaning or reason behind something.
