[c1.name.v2.d00]
The thing has a name. The name is not the thing.

[c1.name.v2.d01]
Called means to name something, or to speak to someone using their name.

[c1.name.v2.d02]
A mark is a sign or shape made on a surface, often to show meaning.

[c1.name.v2.s01]
The name is not the thing. It does not move, it does not feel, it does not breathe. The thing can be alive, but the name is not. Names can change, but the thing stays the same. The word is only a sign. It points to the thing, but it is not the thing.

[c1.name.v2.s02]
The boy drew a picture on a page. The picture showed a tree with green leaves and a red apple. The apple looked real, as if it could fall and land on the ground. He used a blue pen to draw the sky above. The sky was bright, like the sun at noon.

[c1.name.v2.s03]
He used a pen to make a mark. The mark looked like a flower. It was not a real flower. It was a picture of one. The mark meant a flower. The boy knew it was not alive. He could not smell it. He could not touch it. It was not alive.

[c1.name.v2.s04]
The mark meant a flower. A flower was not the thing. The thing had a name. The name was not a flower. The name was real. The name was alive. The name came from the air. The name was not made by hand. The name was not a picture. The name was not a mark.

[c1.name.v2.s05]
The flower was not the thing. The thing was not a flower. The thing was not a name made of air. The thing was not alive in the way a bird is alive. The thing had a shape like a rose. The thing had a name that could not be seen.

[c1.name.v2.s06]
The thing was called a rose. A rose is a name that fits the shape. The name is not the thing, but it helps explain. It is not alive like a bird, but it can be seen. It is not made of air, but it feels real. It is not a flower, yet it looks like one.

[c1.name.v2.s07]
The rose was not the same as the name. The thing was not the same as the name. It could not move, yet it felt alive. It had no breath, but it seemed to grow. It was not a flower, but it looked like one. It was not made of air, but it floated in the wind.

[c1.name.v2.s08]
The boy looked at the page and thought. He could not name the thing, though he tried. The name did not fit, though it felt right. It was not alive, yet it seemed to breathe. It had no face, but it looked at him. It was not a flower, but it grew in the garden.

[c1.name.v2.s09]
The name rose was not the rose. It lived in the garden but was not a flower. It breathed air but was not alive. It had no face but looked at him each night. It was not a bird, but it flew above the trees. It had no body, yet it moved through the grass.

[c1.name.v2.s10]
The name was only a word. It could not be touched but was felt in the air. It had no body but was seen in the garden at night. It was not alive but breathed with the breeze. It was not a dream but came when sleep was near. It was not a thing but held meaning.

[c1.name.v2.s11]
The book held many pages. Each page had a picture and a name. The name was not just a word but a place where meaning came to rest. It was a name that felt like a hand on the back. It was a name that could not be lost even if the pages were torn.

[c1.name.v2.s12]
Each page had a picture and a name. The name was not the thing but a place where meaning came to rest. It was a name that felt like a hand on the back. It was a name that could not be lost even if the pages were torn. The name was alive and knew the shape of the thing.

[c1.name.v2.s13]
The names were not the things. The name was not the thing, but it held the shape of the thing. It lived in the air, above the pages, across the room. It was not alive, yet it knew how to breathe. It could not be lost, even if the book was torn.
