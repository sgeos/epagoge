[p1.change.q1.d00]
Let go and it falls. Open it and it is open.

[p1.change.q1.a01]
What happens when you let go of a ball? It falls because it cannot stay in the air.

[p1.change.q1.a02]
What happens when you close a book? It becomes shut and cannot be read.

[p1.change.q1.a03]
What happens when you cover a box with a cloth? It is hidden and cannot be seen.

[p1.change.q1.a04]
What happens when you drop a cup? It falls to the floor and may break.

[p1.change.q1.a05]
What happens when you open a door? It becomes open and you can go through.

[p1.change.q1.a06]
What happens when you drop a stone into water? It falls through the air and lands in the water.

[p1.change.q1.a07]
What happens when you let go of a balloon? It rises and falls because it has no weight to stay.

[p1.change.q1.a08]
What happens if you let go of a ball? It falls because it drops from your hand.

[p1.change.q1.a09]
What does it mean when something falls? It means it moves down and lands on the ground.

[p1.change.q1.a10]
What happens if you open a door? It becomes open, and you can go through it.

[p1.change.q1.a11]
What does it mean to change? It means to become different from what it was before.

[p1.change.q1.a12]
What happens if you drop a cup? It falls and may land on the floor.

[p1.change.q1.a13]
What happens when something lands? It comes down and rests on the ground.

[p1.change.q1.a14]
What happens when you let go of a ball? It falls because you let go and it falls.

[p1.change.q1.a15]
What happens when you close a book? It becomes closed because you close it and it is closed.
