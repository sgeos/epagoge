[m1.logic.x_emptiness.d00]
If it rains then the ground is wet. All, some, none. It cannot be both so and not so.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s01]
The ground is wet because rain fills the space, and the gap is full, not empty. The space is full because rain fills it, and nothing remains empty. The ground holds water, and the hole is no longer alone. Nothing is lost if it is found, even if it was once hidden.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s02]
Rain falls and fills the hole in the ground, making the space full of water. Rain fills the hole, and the space is no longer empty. The ground holds it, and the hole is full. No one can find the gap, because it is covered. The water is still, and the surface is smooth.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s03]
No one sees the empty space because it is now full of rain. The space is full, not empty, and no one can see the gap. All that remains is water, still and deep, covering the past. The ground holds it, and the surface stays smooth. No one comes to find the spot where nothing was.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s04]
The ground is not empty, and it is not half full, but full. The ground is full, and it was never empty. All that was once missing has been filled by rain. The water holds the shape of what was lost. Even the smallest gap is gone, covered by a smooth surface.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s05]
No one can say it is not full, because the space cannot be both full and empty. All is full, and nothing is empty. No one can be both alive and dead at the same time. The sky is not empty, though it seems so. A child cannot be both asleep and awake.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s06]
The hole cannot be both filled and empty at the same time. Nothing can fill it again because nothing is left to fill. All is gone, and nothing remains. No one can come back to fill it, not even a child. The space above is bright, but it holds no light.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s07]
No one can fill it again because nothing is left to fill. All is gone, and nothing remains. The space above is bright, but it holds no light. No one can come back to fill it, not even a child. The ground is full, and nothing is missing, so none is left.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s08]
The ground is full, and nothing is missing, so none is left. All is still, and no sound moves through the air. Nothing can come again, not even a whisper. The space above holds no light, no breath, no life. No one can enter, not even a shadow. The sky is empty, and no bird flies above.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s09]
No one can doubt it: the space is full, not empty, and the gap is gone. No one can see the gap, though it was once real. All is filled, and nothing remains apart. The space above holds no breath, no sound, no life. No one can enter, not even a shadow.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s10]
Rain falls, the ground fills, the space becomes wet, and no one sees the gap where the dry spot once was. The wet earth holds no breath, no sound, no life. All is filled, and nothing remains apart. The sky above grows dark, the air feels heavy. The ground begins to fill with water from the rain.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s11]
The sky grows dark, the air feels heavy, and the ground begins to fill with water from the rain. All the space below is now full, and nothing remains apart. The water rises slowly, filling every hole, every crack, every small space. Nothing is left outside the flow. Even the sky seems to hold its breath.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s12]
Each drop falls, making a small hole in the dirt, and the hole grows until it is full of water. The water fills the space where dry earth was, and nothing remains empty. The hole is no longer a gap, but a place where liquid holds. No space is left outside the flow, not even above or below.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s13]
The space where dry earth was now holds nothing but liquid, and the ground is no longer empty. No one comes here, not even the bird, because the air is thick with steam and the path is too soft. The water flows slowly, filling every gap, and nothing is left outside the flow.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s14]
Nobody walks there, not even the dog, because the path is too wet and the mud is thick. All is quiet, even the air above feels still. The child does not know what is missing, only that it is not there. The world is full of things that were once alive, now gone.

[m1.logic.x_emptiness.s15]
The child looks down, sees the water, and thinks: nothing can be both dry and wet at once. Nothing is real when the world is full of nothing. All that remains is the sound of breath, slow and steady. The sky is a blue that feels too heavy to hold.
