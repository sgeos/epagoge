[a1.enough.v2.d00]
There is enough water for one plant. There is not enough for two.

[a1.enough.v2.d01]
Enough means having as much as needed, not more, not less.

[a1.enough.v2.d02]
Lack means not having enough of something, like a dry garden.

[a1.enough.v2.d03]
Plenty means more than enough, so there is no shortage.

[a1.enough.v2.d04]
Shortage means not enough of something, like a dry well.

[a1.enough.v2.d05]
Spare means extra, not used, ready if needed.
