# exclude/

**A book here is kept and not trained on.** Move it, do not delete it.

`load_book_dir` reads `*.md` in the level directory and does not descend,
so a book in here is invisible to every generator, validator and training
run while remaining in version control with its history intact.

## What it is for

**Holding a book out of a run without losing it.** A book may be sound and
still wrong for a particular corpus: off-register, redundant with a
better one, or about something a given experiment should not see.
Deleting it destroys the work and the reason; moving it keeps both.

**Accepting outside contributions.** The corpus is CC0 and meant to be
used, so submissions are expected. A submitted book can land here,
be read, and be moved up into the level once it passes. Nothing that
arrives is trained on because it arrived.

## What it is not for

**A book that is wrong should be fixed or deleted**, not parked. A
directory that accumulates work nobody will look at again is a slower way
of deleting it, and the git history already holds anything genuinely
deleted.

**Say why in the commit that moves a book here**, because six months on
the directory cannot say it and neither can the file.
