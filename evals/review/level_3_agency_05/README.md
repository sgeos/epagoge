# Agency scan, fifth pass, level three

Recorded 2026-10-30. 124 headwords from the fifth agency scan, of 214 USGS
Water Science School pages, were scheduled at level three. The sense of
each is past level two, and its concept is already taught. No teacher ran.
The pool, the scan and the level-two decisions are recorded in
`evals/review/level_2_agency_05/`.

114 of the words were in the pool. Constituent, deploy, impoundment,
leach, microorganism, multiply, neutralize, parameter, pathogen and
pressurize were not. The pool held only an inflected form of each, which is
excluded in the level-two review as a form of the citation form.

`tools/measure_lexicon_increment.py` checks growth in distinct headwords
through level two. A level-three admission does not change that count, so
this batch has no `measurement.json`.

`tools/validate_closure.py` on the level-three dictionary reported 3,179
of 3,179 words needing a definition, closure complete, and self-hosting
yes. That check covers words admitted at level three and below. It does
not cover the terms already filed at levels four to six.

Most of these words are terms of water science, chemistry and the body,
such as alkalinity, infiltration, osmosis, turbidity and gastrointestinal.
Each definition was kept short and gives the sense the source uses. The
chemical elements are defined by what they are and where they are found,
without their place in a table of elements, which a later level would add.
Chlorine is defined by its use in making water safe to drink, and arsenic
and methylmercury by being poisons, which is the source's concern with
each.

Multiply is filed here because its concept, multiplication, is first
taught at level three. Induce was drafted for this level and is deferred in
the level-two review, because its form induces is already a headword at
level five.
