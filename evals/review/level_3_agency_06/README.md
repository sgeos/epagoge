# Agency scan, sixth pass, level three

Recorded 2026-10-30. 4 headwords drawn again from the first agency scan of
2026-10-10 were scheduled at level three. The sense of each is past level
two, and its concept is already taught. No teacher ran. The pool and the
level-two decisions are recorded in `evals/review/level_2_agency_06/`.

Comprehensive and incompressibility were in the pool. Dedicate and flux
were not. The pool held only dedicated and fluxes, which are excluded in
the level-two review as forms of the citation forms.

`tools/measure_lexicon_increment.py` checks growth in distinct headwords
through level two. A level-three admission does not change that count, so
this batch has no `measurement.json`.

`tools/validate_closure.py` on the level-three dictionary reported 3,192
of 3,192 words needing a definition, closure complete, and self-hosting
yes. That check covers words admitted at level three and below. It does
not cover the terms already filed at levels four to six.

Incompressibility is defined as how much a substance pushes back when it
is squeezed, after the review changes recorded in the level-two README.
Flux is defined as a flow from one place to
another, which is the sense the source's water cycle tables use.
