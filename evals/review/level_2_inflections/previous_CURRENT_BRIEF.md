Evaluate six manually proposed words from the retained rejection evidence.

the tree fruit with one hard central stone and commonly hairy skin. Plum
denotes the smooth-skinned tree fruit with a central stone. Cherry denotes
the small tree fruit with a central stone. Grape denotes the small fruit
commonly growing in bunches. Lemon denotes the yellow fruit with a sharp
acid taste. These are fruit senses, not tree, colour or figurative senses.
Plant is the existing concept assignment to review for each. Select fruit
explicitly in the defining vocabulary. Other currently admitted defining

Retain a new generation report, candidate sources, every reply, mechanical

## Rationale and limits

Making already admitted defining words visible is a bounded implementation
change with a deterministic verification target. Its effect on teacher output
is unmeasured. A single new batch cannot establish a throughput improvement
or isolate a causal effect. Taste, sugar and sweet remain a later review,
rather than a dependency silently admitted to improve this batch's result.

## Prior failures and wrong turns

Do not equate mechanical acceptance, semantic approval and final admission.
The previous six admissions all required agent adaptations, although one
teacher definition passed the mechanical check. Do not claim that a new
selection option eliminates semantic errors or dictionary cycles.

Do not add selected words to the licensed vocabulary or allow pending
candidates as defining words. Selection changes the prompt, not admission.
Do not increase context or append words beyond the list cap. Validate all
options before teacher access. Retain the existing output protections.

Do not describe every fruit as sweet or edible, reverse a pear's usual shape,
or assume that a colour and size identify a species. Do not label the rough
fruit descriptions in this brief as accepted definitions. Avoid requiring
one colour or shape where real variation exists. A suitable introductory
definition need not be a botanical identification key, but it must convey
the selected meaning without known false necessities.

Keep all previous evidence and uncommitted continuity repairs. Preserve the
523 level-one books and reference weights. Use new artifact paths. Check host
memory and use bounded sequential generation without concurrent training.
Test the new option's failure boundaries without a live teacher. Run the full
gate without weakening checks. Record actual results, not anticipated counts.
No commit or publication is authorized. Ordering is not a completion criterion.
