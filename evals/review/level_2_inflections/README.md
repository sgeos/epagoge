# Level-two inflection review

The review covers all 28 level-two entries without a declared part of speech
on the starting tree. Fifteen entries have repaired declarations and forms.
Thirteen remain deferred with individual reasons in review.json. No new
headwords, concepts or dictionary senses were introduced.

The count nouns reviewed are accident, account, amount, bicycle, bunch,
chain, column, digit, mishap, quantity and system. The verbs are fail,
rearrange, remove and subtract. Existing forms and admission sources remain
unchanged except for the documented additions of forms. Account retains its
reporting sense. Crash is deferred because its definition and stored past
form require a separate noun-and-verb sense review. Number words and other
ambiguous classifications are also deferred.

The review added 14 explicit forms, and direct validator checks show that all
14 also became newly licensed at level two. They are accounts, bicycles,
bunches, failed, failing, mishaps, quantities, rearranges, rearranging,
removes, removing, subtracting, subtracts and systems. The same checks show
no change to their level-one licensing.

Distinct headword counts remain 849 at level one and 964 through level two.
The gap to the approximate ten-thousand-word target remains 9,036. Explicit
term surface forms remain 2,049 at level one and increase from 2,259 to 2,273
through level two. These surface counts exclude standalone core and exempt
items and are not estimates of vocabulary size.

Required definition coverage remains 812 of 812 at level one and 927 of 927
at level two, with complete grounding. The measured hashes preserve all 523
level-one books, the reference checkpoint, the level-two dictionary, and all
prior increment artifacts. Original concepts and source fields are unchanged.

before.json retains the baseline vocabulary, counts and preservation hashes.
review.json records every reviewed entry with before and after states.
measurement.json records the measured outcome and validator outputs. The
baseline and review reproduce the final vocabulary by applying only the
recorded entry changes, a comparison performed during verification.

This was an agent review using existing definitions and the repository's
inflection functions. No teacher generation or training was needed. There is
no independent expert validation and no claim of general throughput or
curriculum benefit. Confirmatory training remains blocked.
