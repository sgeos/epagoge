# Agency scan, fourth pass, level three

Recorded 2026-10-29. 18 headwords from the fourth agency scan, of 147 NASA
Space Place pages, were scheduled at level three. The sense of each is past
level two, and its concept is already taught. No teacher ran. The pool, the
scan and the level-two decisions are recorded in
`evals/review/level_2_agency_04/`.

Seventeen of the words were in the pool. Exoplanet was not. The pool held
only its plural, which is excluded in the level-two review as a form of the
citation form.

`tools/measure_lexicon_increment.py` checks growth in distinct headwords
through level two. A level-three admission does not change that count, so
this batch has no `measurement.json`.

`tools/validate_closure.py` on the level-three dictionary reported 2,699
of 2,699 words needing a definition, closure complete, and self-hosting
yes. That check covers words admitted at level three and below. It does
not cover the terms already filed at levels four to six.

Ion is defined only in its sense of an atom or a particle with an electric
charge, on operator direction. The word charge is admitted at level one in
the sense of being in charge, so the electrical sense rests on the phrase
rather than on a defined sense, as it does for neutron. Transit is defined
by its general sense of passing across, with a planet passing in front of
its star as the example, which is the source's use. Theory is defined as an
explanation that has been tested many times and fits the evidence so far,
and the definition says that new evidence can still show it to be wrong.
