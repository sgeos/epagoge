# Long tail of sixteen sources, level three

Recorded 2026-10-30. 103 headwords and one second sense from the
threshold-four scan of sixteen source texts were scheduled at level three.
The sense of each is past level two, and its concept is already taught. No
teacher ran. The pool, the scan and the level-two decisions are recorded
in `evals/review/level_2_long_tail/`.

74 of the words were in the pool. For 30 others the pool held only an
inflected form, such as arteries, depleted or toxins, which is excluded in
the level-two review as a form of the citation form.

Bond is a second sense. An earlier batch admitted bond at level three as a
noun under force. The sources use bonded for atoms joined together, so the
verb is filed here under joining_and_separating, by word and concept, and
the earlier record and its decision are unchanged.

`tools/measure_lexicon_increment.py` checks growth in distinct headwords
through level two. A level-three admission does not change that count, so
this batch has no `measurement.json`.

`tools/validate_closure.py` on the level-three dictionary reported 3,672
of 3,672 words needing a definition, closure complete, and self-hosting
yes, and found all 302 dictionary records at level three inside the level
and grounded. That check covers words admitted at level three and below.
It does not cover the terms already filed at levels four to six.

Most of these words are terms of science, weather, energy and the body,
such as acidification, catalyst, distillation, filtration, seismometer and
voltage. Each definition was kept short and gives the sense the source
uses. Micron and milligram are defined by their size against a millimeter
and a gram, which are already admitted.

Insolent and plight occur in the McGuffey readers in their current senses,
and are filed here rather than at level two because they are rare in
writing for younger readers.
