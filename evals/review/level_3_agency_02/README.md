# Agency scan, second pass, level three

Recorded 2026-10-28. 32 headwords from the second public-domain agency scan
were scheduled at level three. The sense is past level two, and the concept
is already taught. No teacher ran. The pool, the scan and the level-two
decisions are recorded in `evals/review/level_2_agency_02/`.

28 of these words were in the pool. Digester, kilowatt, photon and
scrubber were not. The pool held only their plurals, which are excluded in
the level-two review as forms of these citation forms.

`tools/measure_lexicon_increment.py` checks growth in distinct headwords
through level two. A level-three admission does not change that count, so
this batch has no `measurement.json`.

`tools/validate_closure.py` on the level-three dictionary reported 2,511
of 2,511 words needing a definition, closure complete, and self-hosting
yes. That check covers words admitted at level three and below. It does
not cover the terms already filed at levels four to six.
