# Agency scan, third pass, level three

Recorded 2026-10-28. 3 headwords from the third public-domain agency scan
were scheduled at level three. The sense is past level two, and the concept
is already taught. No teacher ran. The pool, the scan and the level-two
decisions are recorded in `evals/review/level_2_agency_03/`.

Neutron and quasar were in the pool. Cosmologist was not. The pool held only
its plural, which is excluded in the level-two review as a form of the
citation form.

`tools/measure_lexicon_increment.py` checks growth in distinct headwords
through level two. A level-three admission does not change that count, so
this batch has no `measurement.json`.

`tools/validate_closure.py` on the level-three dictionary reported 2,531
of 2,531 words needing a definition, closure complete, and self-hosting
yes. That check covers words admitted at level three and below. It does
not cover the terms already filed at levels four to six.

The neutron definition says it has no electric charge. The word charge is
admitted at level one in the sense of being in charge, so the electrical
sense rests on the phrase rather than on a defined sense.
