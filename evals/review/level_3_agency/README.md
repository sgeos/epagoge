# Agency scan, level three

Recorded 2026-10-10. 16 headwords from the same public-domain agency scan
were scheduled at level three. The sense is past level two, and the
concept is already taught. No teacher ran.

`tools/measure_lexicon_increment.py` checks growth in distinct headwords
through level two. A level-three admission does not change that count, so
this batch has no `measurement.json`.

`tools/validate_closure.py` on the level-three dictionary reported 2,326
of 2,326 words needing a definition, closure complete, and self-hosting
yes. That check covers words admitted at level three and below. It does
not cover the terms already filed at levels four to six.
